Frostfire Mine:
Skills, Patrols, Veins and Scoring

A 30 minute solo battlefield for Furnace 16 and above. This guide explains the rules and the skill tree first, then gives a practical plan for earning orichalcum.

Overview

What the Event Is

Frostfire Mine is a timed battlefield where every player competes to collect orichalcum. Your score is the total orichalcum you collect, with Smelter bonuses added at the end. The facts below come from several published guides, and we mark the places where they disagree.

🚪
Entry

Several guides give Furnace 16 as the entry level. Check that you can see the event in your Event Center before you plan around it.

⚔️
Format

It is solo player-versus-player, so everyone is an opponent, including members of your own alliance. The match lasts 30 minutes, with a short preparation period before it.

🪖
Troops

Guides report that troops are unlimited in this event and are not permanently lost. Troops need to be in your settlement before you enter, so check this before the start time.

🏆
Score

Orichalcum collected is the score. Patrol guards, veins, outbursts, skill payouts and the Smelter all feed it.

The event is reported to run about every two weeks with several slots a day. Four guides list 02:00, 05:00, 11:00, 14:00, 16:00, 18:00 and 21:00 UTC and one lists only the first four, so read the slots shown in your own Event Center and register before your chosen slot starts.

Battlefield

The Battlefield Zones

The map is three concentric zones. The veins get richer towards the centre, and so does the competition.

Your technology, VIP and item bonuses are reported to stay active. External march speed and gathering speed bonuses reportedly do not, although the speed bonus from the Frostfire skills does. Guides disagree about whether pet and city gathering bonuses apply, so compare the rate shown on your own screen before you rely on one.

Skill Tree

The Five-Tier Skill Tree

You earn XP by defeating Mine Patrols, and XP unlocks skills. The currency is XP, not HP. Each of the five tiers offers a choice between a left and a right branch, and the published costs are 200, 500, 900, 1,500 and 2,300 XP, which is 5,400 in total. One guide says your choices cannot be changed during the match, so decide before you click.

  • Tier 1 (200 XP). Left gives about 1,080 orichalcum per minute. Right gives 1,500 orichalcum for every patrol you defeat.
  • Tier 2 (500 XP). Left adds 12,000 troops per march. Right adds 25 percent battlefield march speed.
  • Tier 3 (900 XP). Left pays 5,000 orichalcum when you occupy a common vein, once per minute. Right pays 5,000 when you fail to occupy a common vein, once per minute.
  • Tier 4 (1,500 XP). Left adds 15 percent gathering efficiency while you occupy a vein. Right cuts hero recovery time by 30 percent.
  • Tier 5 (2,300 XP). Left is a troop damage boost for 60 seconds. Right is a gathering speed boost of 50 percent for 60 seconds. Both have a five minute cooldown.

Read your Tier 5 tooltip. The Right branch is most often given as a 50 percent gathering speed boost for 60 seconds, but one strategy text quotes 66 percent and the Left damage figure is listed inconsistently. The branches themselves are clear: Left is damage and Right is gathering. Use the figure on your own screen.

Five guides give the same general path: Right at Tiers 1, 2 and 5 and Left at Tiers 3 and 4. It makes patrol kills pay and move faster first, then adds the vein payout and gathering efficiency, then ends on the gathering boost. One guide suggests an all-left route for higher-spending accounts. Treat both as starting points that suit a gathering plan, and choose Tier 5 Left if you expect to fight.

Outbursts are reported to appear at minutes 8, 9, 10, 18, 19 and 20, with banners about 60 and 10 seconds beforehand. One guide lists different minutes, so watch for the banners rather than relying on the clock. Because an outburst empties in seconds, there is no five minute hold. Send your marches when the banner appears and move on.

The Smelter sits at the centre of Zone 3 and is reported to open about 23 minutes in, leaving about seven minutes. Published payouts for ranks 1 to 10 are 100,000, 80,000, 60,000, 55,000, 50,000, 40,000, 35,000, 30,000, 25,000 and 20,000 orichalcum. Three guides rank players by occupation time and one by orichalcum collected, so check the ranking rule on the event screen. No guide gives measured results for free-to-play players, and advice differs: one suggests a brief contest, another suggests developing accounts skip it.

The 5,000 Payout

How the Tier 3 Payout Works

Tier 3 Left pays 5,000 orichalcum when you occupy a common vein, once per minute. Players use this by sending one march to a vein, collecting the payout, recalling the march and repeating about once a minute. That is a strategy built on the skill description, and we suggest keeping one march on this loop at a time.

Tier 3 Right pays on a failed occupation. One guide describes recalling a march a few seconds before it finishes occupying to trigger it, but no other guide confirms the method, it needs precise timing and we have not tested it. We have also not confirmed whether simply holding a vein pays the 5,000 more than once. Compare your score over a few minutes both ways in your own game before you commit.

Survival

Heroes, Teleports and Losing a Fight

  • Heroes. A defeated hero is reported to need about five minutes to recover, which is why Tier 4 Right can matter on busy fronts.
  • Teleports. Advanced Teleporters are provided free inside the battlefield, with a cooldown of about six minutes. One guide says extra uses cost increasing items and are capped at five, so use them to leave a depleted area rather than for every small move.
  • Attacking veins. The winner takes the gathering position, and some of the loser's collected orichalcum can be scattered.
  • Leaving. You can rejoin from the event page in the Event Center, but you cannot scout or send troops to the wilderness while you are away.

One guide says Kathy's Expert skills may help with patrol XP, troop capacity and hero recovery here, but it gives no numbers and no other guide mentions it. See the Experts guide and check your own Kathy screen.

Plan

A Suggested Plan

This is our suggested plan for a gathering-focused player, built from the rules above. It is not the only route, and it is not a tested ranking.

  • Start with patrols. Send your marches at Mine Patrols and unlock Tiers 1, 2 and 3 first, which costs 1,600 XP. Early vein gathering pays little compared with what those skills add.
  • Stay on the outskirts to begin with. Fewer players and a steady supply of patrols make it a safer place to build XP. One guide teleports to the centre where patrols cluster, so try both if your outskirts run dry.
  • Add the vein loop once Tier 3 is active. One march runs the occupy, collect and recall loop. The rest stay on patrols while you unlock Tier 4 and then Tier 5.
  • Use outbursts when the banner appears. Send marches in at once, collect and return. Do not plan a long hold.
  • Keep killing patrols to the end. With Tier 1 Right each defeat pays 1,500 orichalcum right up to the final second.
  • Treat the Smelter as optional. Decide based on your power, how many marches you can afford to lose and how late in the match you are.
Community Feedback

What Players Report

These are player and guide opinions, not guaranteed best practice. Several of them contradict each other, which is why we list them rather than pick one.

  • Right, Right, Left, Left, Right is the common route. Five guides use it for general and free-to-play play. One suggests all-left for bigger spenders.
  • Outskirts or centre. Some players farm quietly on the outskirts. Others teleport to the centre where patrols cluster. Both are described as workable.
  • Smelter or no Smelter. Some guides say even a short occupation adds to your score. Others tell developing accounts to avoid it.
  • The recall trick. One guide presents the failed-occupation recall as a standard technique. No other source backs it, so treat it as unverified.
  • Projected totals. Some guides quote expected scores for perfect play. They give no basis, so use your own results to judge your plan.

Rewards listed by guides include Charm Designs and Charm Guides, Gems, Avatar Frames, resource chests, speedups and cosmetics. Guides show rank and milestone thresholds only as images, so check your own rewards screen.

Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Choosing Skill 5 from a guide. Read both branches on your own screen. Left is damage and Right is gathering.

Rushing the Smelter at the start. It opens late, and marches you lose early cost you patrol XP.

Gathering veins before Tier 3 is unlocked. Early marches do more good on patrols while XP is still unlocking your payouts.

Planning a five minute outburst hold. Outbursts are reported to empty in about 20 seconds.

Assuming normal gathering bonuses apply. Guides say many external bonuses do not apply inside the battlefield.

Stopping patrol kills early. If you took Tier 1 Right, every kill pays until the final second.

Treating projected scores as targets. They come from unverified guides.

Check on your own screens. The event slots for your state, the Tier 5 figures, the Smelter opening time and ranking rule, the outburst banners, teleport costs and whether holding a vein repeats the 5,000 payout. Guides were written for different seasons, so exact figures can change.
