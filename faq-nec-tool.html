NEC Contract Manager: Your Questions Answered

What the tool tracks, how it works on site without a connection and who it is built for.

What It Does

What is the NEC Contract Manager?

A browser-based tool for tracking Early Warning Notices (EWNs) and compensation events (CEs) through the NEC4 and NEC3 assessment cycle. It replaces the mix of spreadsheets, paper forms and email threads that many projects use to keep track of these.

Where did it come from?

Atker Consultancy Services came to Digital Revenue Studio with an NEC process being tracked across spreadsheets, paper and email. We built them a browser-based tool from scratch. They use it, and it is now available to anyone with the same problem.

What does the dashboard show?

Open items, deadlines and warnings in one place, so you can see what needs attention without searching through registers. Time bar warnings are part of that.

Which NEC versions does it cover?

NEC4 and NEC3. The EWN register follows NEC4 clause 15.1 and NEC3 clause 16.1.

Using It On Site

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. It is a browser-based file with nothing to install, and it works offline on site once it is downloaded.

What is the NEC Field Notification Form?

A mobile-first form for issuing EWNs and notifying compensation events from site. It is designed for phones and tablets, with large tap targets, and you can attach photo evidence from your camera roll and produce a PDF ready to email from site.

Can I save it to my phone like an app?

Yes. You can save it to your phone's home screen for app-like access. There is no app store listing and no software licence to approve.

Can I export notices?

Yes. One-click PDF export produces professional notices you can send straight to the other party.

Is It Right for You?

Does it replace contract advice?

No. It is a tracking and notification tool. It helps you record and manage notices on time, but it does not replace a commercial manager, a project manager or professional advice on how a contract should be interpreted.

Is it a subscription?

No. There is no subscription and nothing to install. See the tool page for the current options and the best value bundle.

Is there a CDM tool as well?

Yes. The CDM Digital Audit Tool covers CDM 2015 compliance with a 45-item checklist. It has its own FAQ.
