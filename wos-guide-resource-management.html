Resource Strategy

Resource Management
for F2P Players

How to manage coal, wood, meat and iron: production buildings, offline gathering, the heroes who boost each resource and how to protect what you hold.

Fire Crystals are different. Guides say they cannot be gathered and come from events, the alliance shop, packs and some daily tasks. They are needed for Furnace levels above 30. See the Server Timeline for when they arrive.

Coal and Heat

Coal and the Furnace

The Furnace burns coal to keep your city warm, and its energy meter shows heat. Guides say low heat makes survivors ill, which slows gathering and building. We could not find a source that lists exactly what happens when coal runs out, so keep a healthy reserve and check your own screen.

One guide says there is a Max Power setting. It advises using it only during an extreme cold warning, because otherwise the Furnace burns too much coal. The same guide says keeping survivor happiness above 90 percent gives extra resources, without giving an amount. Earlier versions of this guide described a manual setting for lower power in the daytime. We could not find a source for that, so we removed it. Look at the Furnace controls in your own game.

Production

Making Your City Work Harder

  • Staff your buildings. Guides say to assign survivors to production buildings. An unstaffed building produces less, and checking assignments now and then is worth the time.
  • Level them evenly. Higher building levels mean higher output. One guide advises upgrading all production buildings of one type evenly and treating them as lower priority than the Furnace, military buildings and the Research Center. Production alone will not meet your needs at higher levels.
  • Use boosts. Guides list items and VIP as ways to raise production, and suggest the Development and Economy research trees early. Check your own research screen for resource effects.
Gathering

The World Map Is Your Main Supply

Gathering on the world map is a major source of resources, and it keeps running while you are offline. Both guides we read imply this, though neither gives rates or caps.

  • Send marches before you log off. Start gathers before you sleep so the marches are working while you are away. One guide warns that gathering troops can be attacked, so use a shield if you need one.
  • Match the node to your march. Higher-level nodes hold more but need more troops and time. We found no published yields, so compare what your marches bring back.
  • Use alliance territory. Guides say alliance territory gives faster gathering and better protection from random attacks, and that the alliance Territory tech includes gathering speed. No numbers are published.
Gathering Heroes

Which Heroes Help Gathering

The Rare Growth heroes each raise one resource. Their Expedition skills increase both the output of the matching city building and the gathering speed for that resource on the map, by 5 to 25 percent depending on skill level. These come from our hero data.

Earlier versions named Gina as a gathering hero. Her skills reduce stamina cost and raise wilderness march speed, which suits hunting, not gathering. We also could not find any hero called Yuka, so we removed that name. See our Hero Priority guide before spending shards on any of them.

Protection

Keeping Raiders Out

Guides name the Storehouse as the building that protects a portion of your resources from plunder, up to Level 30. They do not give the protected amounts. Peace shields prevent attacks on your city for their duration, and guides say offline players are common targets when no shield is active.

  • Spend promptly. Resources above your Storehouse limit can be plundered, so spend them on upgrades and training instead of leaving large stockpiles.
  • Use a shield when it matters. If you hold a lot and will be offline, a shield stops attacks on your city while it lasts.
  • Crates. One guide says unopened items are completely safe and become vulnerable once opened. Another says to take resources from the backpack only when you need them. We could not confirm the details, so check in game.
  • Stay with an active alliance. Guides say alliance territory also offers some protection.
Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Running out of coal. Keep a reserve and check it often.

Idle marches overnight. Send marches before you log off.

Large unprotected stockpiles. Spend them or protect them.

Leaving buildings unstaffed. Check survivor assignments.

Naming the wrong gathering hero. Check the hero's own skills before you invest.

Check on your own screens. The Furnace controls, your Storehouse capacity, the yield of the nodes you gather from, the effect of each hero skill and what happens to your city when coal is low. The details here come from published guides and our hero data, and some could not be confirmed.
