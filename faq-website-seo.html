FAQ Websites & SEO

Small Business Websites and SEO: Your Questions Answered

What a new site needs to be found on Google, what you can do for free and what to expect.

Building the Site

Do I need to pay for hosting?

Not at launch. We build static, fast sites on free hosting and move to paid options only when a business needs more. A static site is quick to load, secure by default and cheap to run.

Why does mobile-first matter?

Most visitors arrive on a phone, and Google judges the mobile version of your page first. A site that looks good on a laptop but is awkward on a phone will lose both visitors and rankings.

What is a canonical tag?

A line in the page that tells search engines which URL is the main version of the page. It stops duplicate versions of the same page competing with each other.

Getting Found on Google

What is a sitemap and do I need one?

A sitemap is a file listing the pages you want search engines to find. Yes, you need one. It helps Google discover new pages and understand how your site fits together.

What is Google Search Console?

A free Google tool that shows how your site appears in search, which pages are indexed and what problems Google found. Submitting your sitemap there is one of the first steps after launch.

What are breadcrumbs for?

Breadcrumbs show where a page sits on your site, for example Home, then FAQ, then a topic. They help visitors move around and help search engines understand your structure. We add them with structured data so Google can show them in results.

Why do FAQ pages help?

They answer real questions in plain language, which is how many people search. Each FAQ page gives Google a clear topic to match, and links to related pages that spread that relevance across the site.

What to Expect

How long does SEO take?

Months, not days. A new site has to be discovered, crawled and trusted. Content that is useful and kept up to date compounds over time, which is why we plan content as part of a build and not as an afterthought.

Can you promise a first page ranking?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We can set up the foundations properly and publish content that deserves to rank. Results depend on competition, topic and time.
