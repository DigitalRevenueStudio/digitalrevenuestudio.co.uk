How we work

How we write and check our guides.

Every guide on this wiki is written in our own words, checked against what the game shows and labelled with how far that checking has gone. This page explains the process, what each label means and how to tell us when something is wrong.

The process

From a question to a published guide.

01

Research

We start from what the game itself shows: our own accounts, the in-game event and building screens, and the game's published information. Player reports from alliances and community discussions add experience, and we label them as community reports so you can tell them apart from things we have confirmed.

02

Write

Each guide is written in our own words for free to play and low spending players. Where a number or mechanic is not confirmed, we say so and point you to the screen in your own game that shows the real value.

03

Review

Alliance members read the guide and check the numbers, names and mechanics. Reviewers are named at the bottom of the guide, and the status at the top shows how far the review has got.

04

Update

Each guide shows when it was last updated. When the game changes or someone reports a mistake, we correct the guide, change its date and note the change on the news page.

Review labels

What the labels mean.

Every guide and tool carries one of these labels, so you can see how far it has been checked before you rely on it.

Community reviewed

Checked by players

Alliance members have read the guide and checked its numbers, names and mechanics. It can still change when the game does.

Review in progress

Live, still being checked

We have written and checked the guide, but the reviewers have not finished with it. Treat details as likely rather than certain and check your own screen.

Awaiting review

Not yet reviewed

The guide is live but has not yet been through community review.

Beta

Still being tested

Used for tools such as the Hero Advisor and the Bear Generator. Their answers are suggestions to weigh, not official game results or damage simulations.

Details to be confirmed

Not guessed

The game has released something but reliable details are not yet available, so we have not filled the gap with guesses.

Gift code labels

How sure we are

Verified active means we redeemed the code recently. Reported active and reported expired mean players and code sites say it works or has ended, and we have not re-tested it. Expired means confirmed as no longer redeemable.

Our approach

What we do and what we do not do.

We write for free to play and low spending players, so we cover what costs nothing first and say plainly where spending changes the result. When sources disagree, for example on when a feature arrives on a new server, we show the range and explain the difference instead of picking one number and presenting it as fact.

We do not copy text or artwork from the game or from other sites. Hero stats and skill values are published game information, which we show in tables of our own. The explanations, ratings and advice around them are our own writing, and our recommendations are suggestions, not guarantees.

This wiki is an unofficial fan resource. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's publisher. The tools on it, including the Hero Advisor and the Bear Generator, are planning aids in beta, and they do not connect to your game account.

Keeping guides current

We keep a public record of what we have checked, what we have corrected and what is still open on the checks and corrections page. The date on a guide shows when it was last changed. It does not mean every line was re-checked that day. Gift code labels are reviewed as often as we can manage, and each code shows its own status. Larger guides are read again when a game update changes what they describe, and we record the change on the news page.

Telling us about a mistake

If something is wrong, out of date or missing, use the form on the community hub. We read every message, check it against the game where we can and correct the guide. Corrections are usually made within a day, and the guide's date is updated when we do.
