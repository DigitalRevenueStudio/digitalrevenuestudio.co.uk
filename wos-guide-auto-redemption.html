Gift codes in Whiteout Survival are one of the best free resources available to F2P players. Speed-ups, resources, hero fragments: codes hand them out without asking for anything in return. The problem is keeping track of them, remembering to redeem them, and making sure you do not miss one while you are offline.

WOS Rewards is a third-party tool built to take that chore away, and for most players it is one of those small quality-of-life upgrades that simply works.

What Is It

What Is WOS Rewards?

WOS Rewards (wosrewards.com) is a third-party site that tracks Whiteout Survival gift codes. Its free tool lets you enter your Player ID and redeem all currently active codes to your account in one go. There is no manual input and no hunting through social media for the latest codes.

✓ Free Tier: For Individual Players
Enter your Player ID and redeem all active codes instantly. No registration required. The full core value of the tool, completely free.
★ Paid Tier: For Alliance Leaders
Auto-redeems codes across your entire roster. Built for R4/R5 leaders managing 50 to 100+ players. Roughly $10 to $24 a month depending on size when we last checked in March 2026, so confirm current pricing on the site.
Free Tier

What the Free Tier Does for You

The free tier is all most players need. You enter your Player ID, and the site redeems all active codes straight to your account. No typing codes in one by one through the in-game interface, and no worrying about whether you have missed something that dropped overnight.

The real benefit is peace of mind. Codes come out at all hours: during live streams, from creator partnerships, around update windows. Without a tool like this it is easy to keep second-guessing whether you have caught everything. With it, you check the site, it handles the redemption, and the rewards show up.

For a F2P player where every speed-up and resource matters, that is not a small thing.

Yes, use the free tier. It removes the manual hassle of code redemption and makes sure you are not leaving free resources on the table. It earns its place in any F2P routine. The paid tier is for alliance leaders managing a roster, and individual players do not need it.

Join the Telegram for alerts, run the site when new codes drop, and stop worrying about missing out.
