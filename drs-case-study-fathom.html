Fathom Guides:
A game guide brand, built route by route

A new independent publisher of downloadable game guides. Brand, content and a live-updating website, launched with four Whiteout Survival guides and a Minecraft manual in production.

The approach

One brand. Many games.

Fathom Guides is an independent brand that publishes practical game guides as downloadable PDFs. The name and the compass logo are deliberately game-neutral, so the same identity can cover mobile, console and PC titles without being tied to any single one.

The guides themselves follow one rule: they are written to be used while the game is open. Each is a route you follow in order, with a checkpoint at the end of every chapter, and the numbers inside are checked against the current version of the game.

“A route, not a wiki.” (Fathom Guides)

Digital Revenue Studio is the branding and content partner behind the brand. Fathom is independent, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Century Games, Mojang Studios or Microsoft.
