About the brand

One brand, many games

Fathom Guides is an independent brand that publishes practical game guides as downloadable PDFs. The name and the compass logo are deliberately game-neutral, so one identity can cover mobile, console and PC titles.

Digital Revenue Studio is the branding and content partner behind the brand. Fathom is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Century Games, Mojang Studios, Microsoft, Supercell or Nintendo.

The idea

A route, not a wiki

Fathom guides follow one rule: they are written to be used while the game is open. Each is a route you follow in order, with a checkpoint at the end of every chapter, and the numbers inside are checked against the current version of the game.
