SvS Research Day:
Day 2 or Day 5?

Move between two alliances and you can hear two different answers to the same question. One says spend research speed-ups on Day 2. The other says hold them for Day 5. Both usually have a reason, and the difference comes down to strategy and timing.

The Two Days

What Each Day Offers

Day 2 of SvS prep is the research day. Research speed-ups and Fire Crystal Shard research score there, alongside the Lucky Wheel and hero shards, according to the guides we read. Day 5 is the widest scoring day of the week. It covers pets, Chief Gear and Essence Stones, and two guides say speed-ups of every type score equally on it, research included.

Why Alliances Split

The President Buff Is the Real Question

Some alliances time their research around a buff the President can activate. We found no published guide that explains how it works, and one guide says any active President buff ends when the battle phase starts, so treat it as an alliance plan and not a rule. Some states use Day 2, which matches the research day. Others hold their research for Day 5, which is the widest scoring window.

That is why your old alliance and your new one can both be right. They are not disagreeing about the rules. They are working from a different plan for when to spend.

What To Do

How to Decide

Ask first: Ask your R4 or R5 which day your alliance plans to spend research speed-ups, and follow that plan.

Check the event list: The prep schedule can change between seasons. Look at the in-game event list on Monday before you spend anything.

Follow your current alliance: If you have moved alliances, go with the new one, even if the old one did it differently. Coordinated alliance rewards and the state plan matter more than a small difference in points.

We have updated the SvS Prep Guide to reflect both approaches. It previously leaned on Day 5 for research, which was too narrow.
