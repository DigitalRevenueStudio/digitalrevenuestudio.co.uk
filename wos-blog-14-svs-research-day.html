SvS Research Day:
Day 2 or Day 5?

Move between two alliances and you can hear two different answers to the same question. One says spend research speed-ups on Day 2. The other says hold them for Day 5. Both usually have a reason, and the difference comes down to strategy and timing.

The Two Days

What Each Day Offers

Day 2 of SvS prep is the research day. Research speed-ups, Fire Crystal Shards, Expert Sigils and Books of Knowledge all score there, alongside the Lucky Wheel and hero shards. Day 5 is the widest scoring day of the week. It covers pets, Chief Gear and Essence Stones, and in many seasons it also accepts most speed-up types, research included.

Why Alliances Split

The President Buff Is the Real Question

The President can activate a Research Advancement buff, and the usual plan is to spend research speed-ups while it is running. Some states fire it on Day 2 so it lines up with the research day. Others hold it for Day 5. Day 2 already produces a lot of points from the Lucky Wheel and hero shards, so those states save the buff and the research push for the last stretch of prep, when matchups tend to be decided.

That is why your old alliance and your new one can both be right. They are not disagreeing about the rules. They are working from a different plan for when the buff lands.

What To Do

How to Decide

Ask first: Ask your R4 or R5 which day your President plans to use the research buff. Spend research speed-ups on that day.

Check the event list: The prep schedule can change between seasons. Look at the in-game event list on Monday before you spend anything.

Follow your current alliance: If you have moved alliances, go with the new one, even if the old one did it differently. Coordinated alliance rewards and the state plan matter more than a small difference in points.

We have updated the SvS Prep Guide to reflect both approaches. It previously leaned on Day 5 for research, which was too narrow.
