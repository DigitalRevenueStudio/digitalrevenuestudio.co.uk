How we make money

We want you to know exactly where our income comes from, so you can judge our content fairly. Digital Revenue Studio earns money in four ways:

  • Studio services, such as the Branded Content Pack, which clients pay for directly.
  • Digital products, such as Prompt Studio Plus and the Tools Directory, sold through Payhip, and tools for our client brands sold through Gumroad.
  • Affiliate links to a small number of products and services, explained below.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links. If you click one and then buy or sign up, we may earn a commission. It never costs you anything extra. Affiliate links are marked on the page where they appear, and we add rel="sponsored" to them so search engines can see them too.

Our current affiliate and referral relationships are:

  • Amazon Associates (book and product recommendations, mainly on Mavericks Adventures pages).
  • Payhip (seller referral programme).

We only recommend things we use, have used, or have looked at closely enough to stand behind. A commission never decides what we say in a review. Some of the tools we write about, such as Sender, are recommended without any affiliate arrangement at all, and we say so on those pages.
