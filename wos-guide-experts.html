Experts System:
Complete F2P Guide

The Experts system unlocks in the mid game and provides permanent passive bonuses across your city, combat and event performance. Knowing which Expert to invest in first, which to skip and how Sigils work is one of the most debated topics in WOS. This guide covers all 11 Experts, including the newest additions Gareth and Justus, with priority order and F2P strategy.

Overview

How the Experts System Works

Experts are NPC characters who provide permanent passive bonuses across your city, combat and event performance. The feature launched in August 2025 and requires Furnace Level 25 to unlock. Experts are unlocked through two routes: Tundra Trek (the F2P path, longer grind but fully accessible) and Frontier Trek (the faster pay-to-play path). Once invited to your city, Experts are upgraded at the Dawn Academy using Books of Knowledge and Expert Sigils.

There are currently 11 Experts, arriving in waves as your state progresses. Generation numbers here follow the usual community numbering for the earlier Experts. Players disagree about where Gareth and Justus fit, so we name them as newer Experts and not by generation. Check in game if it matters to a decision. Costs grow exponentially as you level up, so planning your investment order before you start spending is important.

Upfront note: Expert priorities are one of the most debated topics in WOS. The right order genuinely depends on your playstyle, alliance activities and what events you participate in. This guide presents the most common F2P consensus as a starting framework, not a fixed rule. Ask experienced players in your alliance what they prioritise and why.

Generation 1 Experts

Unlocks at ~Day 150 (Furnace 25, Fire Crystal Lv. 5)

Agnes

Agnes reduces construction time (by up to ~8 hours per upgrade at higher levels), provides daily Seeker Chests and grants extra Intel missions (roughly 1 to 3 additional Intel missions per day depending on skill level). This matters more than it first appears. Faster construction means faster Fire Crystal progression, which gates your troop tiers, Furnace levels and late-game unlocks. More Intels means more rewards and more widgets, and widgets directly scale hero combat power alongside star level. Players who underinvest in Agnes often find their progression quietly bottlenecked across multiple systems. She is considered easy to max and high priority for virtually all F2P players.

Breakpoint: Push Agnes to Casual 2 as quickly as possible. After that, steady ongoing investment. Her skill upgrades remain worthwhile throughout the game.

Cyrille

Cyrille is the Bear Huntress. Her five Bear Hunt skills cover rewards, capacity and personal damage points. Weapon Master (extra Essence Stones) and Scavenging (extra Enhancement XP Components) pay out every session, Entrapment adds rally capacity for the account launching the rally, Ursa's Bane adds deployment capacity, and Hunter's Heart raises your own damage points. Essence Stones and Enhancement XP support Hero Gear, so her reward skills are the main targets for a regular joiner. See the Bear Hunt guide for the full breakdown.

Breakpoint: Develop Cyrille alongside Agnes rather than leaving her entirely. Aim for Weapon Master and Scavenging first. A good stopping point is Casual 1 initially, returning once Agnes is at Casual 2.

Holger

Holger focuses on Arena. He provides large Arena Shop discounts (up to ~50% off) and combat attribute bonuses for your Heroes. One active player reported gaining an extra 1 to 3 Mythic Shards daily and significantly more gear XP with Holger well-developed. Several experienced players rate him very highly because Mithril and Mythic gear are among the biggest mid-to-late game bottlenecks, and Holger is the most direct route to getting more of them. Skills 2 and 3 are the priority. Affinity Level 50 is considered a strong stopping point for F2P before costs become too high.

Romulus

Romulus provides general troop stat boosts and increases both global rally capacity and troop capacity. He also provides free daily troops at higher levels. He is one of the most expensive Experts to upgrade and provides little return for casual F2P players. However, the picture changes as your server matures. In established late-game alliances, Romulus becomes genuinely valuable for anyone regularly leading SvS rallies, Fortress stacking or Foundry Battle. His rally capacity increases compound meaningfully at that level of play. For early-to-mid game F2P players, the community consensus is to leave him until all other Experts are well-developed.

F2P note: Skip Romulus or deprioritise heavily in the early and mid game. He becomes more relevant later, particularly for R4/R5 players and regular rally leaders in competitive alliances.

Generation 2 Experts

Unlocks at ~Day 195 (Hero Generation 4)

Baldur

Baldur is the Expert for alliance event income, and for most F2P players he belongs near the top of the order. His skills raise Alliance Mobilization points (Blazing Sunrise) and add Championship Badges plus extra Alliance Championship shop items (Honored Conquest), including Books of Knowledge and a small number of Common Expert Sigils. That makes him a useful way to top up your other Experts without paying, although players disagree on exactly how much. We have not confirmed a fixed weekly amount, and it probably depends on his skill level, so check the skill description and your actual shop rewards in game. He also boosts points and rewards in alliance events including Alliance Championship and Crazy Joe.

Baldur feeds the rest of your Expert plan. The Books of Knowledge and Sigils he adds to the Championship shop help fund Agnes, Cyrille and the others, but they are limited, so treat it as a steady top-up and not a Sigil factory. Save Books of Knowledge and Sigils ahead of his unlock so you can invest immediately on arrival.

Breakpoint: Develop Honored Conquest and Blazing Sunrise early, ahead of his other skills. Then continue steady investment.

Fabian

Fabian earns additional tokens from the Foundry Battle shop and has a skill that instantly heals a set number of troops during Foundry Battle (players report healing significant troop numbers without leaving the event for the usual ~12 minute hospital wait). His other skills are mainly useful for rally leaders. If you participate regularly in Foundry Battle, he has meaningful value. If you do not, he can be deprioritised significantly. Level 60 gets most of his useful skills to a high level and is a reasonable stopping point before costs become excessive.

Valeria

Valeria boosts SvS points, provides State of Power Tokens (used to buy Fire Crystals and Shards, typically worth several hundred tokens per SvS cycle depending on skill level) and increases SvS rally capacity. Her second skill occasionally offers Expert Sigils in her shop, which should always be bought when available. Her capacity and stat boost skills are generally considered low value for F2P. She is worth investing in once Gen 1 Experts are well-developed. Save Sigils and Books of Knowledge ahead of her unlock.

Ronne

Ronne is a legendary mercenary added in March 2026. She specialises in the Tundra Trade Route event, providing attack and defence bonuses for truck raiding, enhanced cargo gathering and additional daily truck refreshes. She belongs with the Generation 2 Experts, not Generation 3 as an earlier version of this page said. Based on community signals, she is a niche, event-specific Expert with limited use outside a monthly event. She is good for squeezing more resources from the Trade Route as F2P, but her design positions her as a later priority. Invest in her after the broader recurring-income Experts are well developed.

Newer Experts

Kathy, Gareth and Justus (2026)

Kathy

Kathy is the Mine Leader, added in April 2026 with a focus on the Frostfire Mine event. Her value depends on how seriously you play that event. Two of her skills (Winter Treasures and part of Efficient Mining) unlock their best rewards once you reach about 200,000 Orichalcum in a Frostfire Mine, and those rewards are Chief Charm materials, namely Charm Designs and Charm Guides. Her other skills help with Mine Patrol XP, capacity and recovery. Frostfire Mine runs every two weeks, so a player who regularly reaches the Orichalcum threshold sees repeated value, while a player who does not will see far less. Develop her once the core reward Experts are in good shape, if you actively play Frostfire Mine.

Gareth

Gareth is the Ironthorn Chief, a survivability specialist who arrives around state day 270 to 280 (players report slightly different days, and some report a staged rollout, so not every state or player gets him on the same day). His most useful skill for F2P players is Gifts of Iron, which gives Ironwrought Chests as you train troops, up to a daily limit. The chests contain speedups and a small chance of Fire Crystals. His other skills raise troop Defence and Health by up to about 50% each and trim enemy Lethality slightly, and his talent, Rallying Cry, adds Infirmary capacity and faster healing. Those combat skills are expensive. His full kit costs over 1.3 million Books of Knowledge (roughly 73,500 for Justus) and about 2,700 Sigils, more than Romulus, while Gifts of Iron on its own is about 13,500 Books.

Suggested approach: Develop Gifts of Iron for the recurring chests, then pause unless combat is a major goal for your account. Maxing Gareth is not a standard F2P target.

Justus

Justus is an account-progression Expert rather than a combat one. He became available to eligible states from 26 October 2026, around state day 365, so not every state has him yet. Players disagree on which generation he belongs to and which hero generation gates his arrival, so we have not put a generation number on him. His main value is Dynasty Honor Chests, earned through Exploration Rewards, beast rallies and Trek encounters, each with a daily limit. The chests hold Expert development materials: Books of Knowledge, Learning Speedups and a small chance of Common Expert Sigils. His other skills boost Labyrinth shop currency and add Pet Adventure attempts with a chance of double rewards.

Suggested approach: Aim first for Relationship Level 40 (Casual 1), which unlocks all four upgradeable skills for roughly 80 Justus Sigils in total, then put the chest-producing skills first. Early Books of Knowledge go furthest on Master Diplomat and Fortune Wayfarer. He deserves investment before optional event specialists and expensive combat upgrades.

Community feedback on Justus: Feedback from the community offers a slightly different view, so we are showing it alongside ours. The skill notes shared with us say that the first skill adds roughly 4 to 20% more Labyrinth shop currency across its levels, the second gives roughly 15 to 35% double rewards on Pet Adventure plus an extra daily attempt, the third gives Dynasty Chests from Polar Terror rallies each day, and the fourth gives them from daily Expert Treks. The same feedback suggests building towards Casual 3 (level 60), which it puts at roughly 156K Expert XP and around 168 Sigils to max his skills, and putting your first Books into skills 2 and 3, with skill 1 next and skill 4 at your own pace if you do Expert Treks daily. Rough Book costs given were about 12K, 15K, 15K and 21K for skills 1 to 4. Our Casual 1 figure above is about unlocking all four skills, the feedback is about maxing them, so the two may fit together. Figures vary between players, so check in game and try what works for your account.

A note on the figures: The skill names and figures for Kathy, Gareth and Justus come from community feedback and may be rounded. Generation numbers and some costs vary between players, so check in game if a figure matters to a decision.

Priority Order

Where to Start

This order prioritises recurring rewards and account growth for F2P players. It does not mean you should max each Expert before starting the next. Develop useful reward skills, reach an affordable stopping point, then reassess. After the core economy Experts, your event participation should determine where you invest next. It is our editorial recommendation, not a universal ranking, and not every state has every Expert yet, so skip anyone who has not unlocked for you.

Two things to note: Justus is fourth in the default list, so do not wait until the first three are finished. His relationship rewards and free starting skill levels justify starting him as soon as you are eligible. Gareth is different: his reward skill and his combat kit sit in different categories. Gifts of Iron is a relatively small investment, while his whole kit costs over 1.3 million Books of Knowledge, compared with roughly 73,500 for Justus. Full Gareth development belongs near the bottom of a general F2P plan.

If Arena matters to you: Some experienced players rank Holger above Agnes because Mithril and Mythic gear are the biggest power bottleneck in the mid-to-late game. If pushing Arena performance is your goal, consider moving Holger higher in your personal order.

SvS timing note: Some players recommend saving Books of Knowledge and Expert Sigils specifically for SvS Day 2 and Day 3 when Expert upgrade scoring is active. Others argue the long-term value of upgrading Experts immediately outweighs the one-time SvS scoring benefit. Both positions have merit, check what your alliance recommends.

Breakpoints

When to Stop and Save

Expert upgrade costs grow exponentially. Knowing where to pause investment and redirect resources is as important as knowing where to start. These are the key stopping points the community has identified:

Agnes: Push to Casual 2 as fast as possible. After that, steady ongoing investment is worthwhile throughout the game.

Cyrille: Weapon Master and Scavenging first. Casual 1 initially, return after Agnes reaches Casual 2.

Holger: Pause at Affinity Level 50. Skills 2 and 3 first. The return on investment up to this point is extremely high. Beyond Level 50 costs escalate sharply relative to benefit.

Baldur: Invest in his Championship and Mobilisation skills (Honored Conquest and Blazing Sunrise) as soon as he unlocks, then continue steady development.

Fabian: Level 60 gets most of his useful skills to a solid level and is a reasonable stopping point. Beyond this costs climb steeply.

Justus: Relationship Level 40 (Casual 1) first, then the chest-producing skills. Higher levels need investment across several of his skills, so plan ahead before committing.

Gareth: Develop Gifts of Iron, then pause. His combat skills cost far more than the core reward Experts.

F2P Strategy

Getting the Most from Limited Resources

Expert upgrade costs grow exponentially. Going deep on your priority Experts rather than spreading resources thin is more efficient than trying to level everyone evenly. Sigils are the scarce currency and the free sources are limited: shop offers such as Baldur's Championship shop items and Valeria's shop, plus a small chance from Justus's chests. Spend them on skills that return something every week.

Save Sigils and Books of Knowledge ahead of Gen 2 Expert unlocks (Baldur and Valeria) so you can invest in them quickly on arrival. The same logic applies to Justus when he reaches your state: arrive with Books of Knowledge ready.

Community note: Expert priority is one of the most debated topics in WOS and the right answer depends on your playstyle and goals. The order above is our recommended starting point, but your alliance's R4/R5 will have views based on your specific server meta. Ask them before committing significant Books of Knowledge to any Expert.

Common Mistakes

What Slows Your Expert Progress

Spreading Books of Knowledge across every Expert evenly. This is the single most common mistake. Upgrading every Expert a little at a time means none of them reach the skill breakpoints that deliver real value. Go deep on 1 to 2 Experts first rather than spreading thin across all of them.

Ignoring Baldur or delaying him. Players who do not prioritise Baldur's Championship and Mobilisation skills miss a steady stream of alliance event rewards, including Books of Knowledge and a few Sigils from the Championship shop. Every week you delay is a week of that income you cannot recover.

Investing heavily in Romulus too early. Romulus costs significantly more than other Experts at equivalent levels and the return for F2P players in the early and mid game is low. Money spent on Romulus before Agnes, Cyrille, Holger and Baldur are well-developed is almost always a suboptimal use of resources.

Not saving ahead of Gen 2 unlocks. Baldur and Valeria unlock around Day 195 and both benefit from immediate investment. Players who have not saved Sigils and Books of Knowledge ahead of this point often have to wait weeks before they can activate the skills that matter most.

Skipping Holger because Arena feels optional. Arena provides Mithril and Mythic gear through its shop. At higher skill levels Holger provides discounts of roughly up to ~50% in that shop. Players who deprioritise Holger often find gear progression becoming their primary bottleneck in the mid-to-late game, typically around Day 200 onwards.

Spending Books of Knowledge outside of SvS without a plan. Some players spend as soon as they accumulate resources. Others save specifically for SvS scoring windows. Both approaches have merit, but spending blindly without knowing which approach suits your alliance is a common source of regret.
