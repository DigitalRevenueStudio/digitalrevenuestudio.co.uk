About this page

What this record covers.

This page lists what we have confirmed on our own screens, what we changed after checking, and what we are still unsure about. It started on 30 October 2026 and covers the accuracy review we carried out on the wiki that month. Changes made before the review were not recorded here.

We list only what we can stand behind. Where we have not been able to check something, we say so. For how a guide is researched and labelled, see how we write and check our guides, and for the status of each guide, see the community review page.

Confirmed on our own screens

What we have seen in the game.

These checks were made on our own accounts. The game changes, so treat each one as true on the date shown and check your own screen.

GuideWhat we checkedWhat our screen showedChecked
Drill CampLevel sharing, slots, cooldown and resetsHeroes in a Drill Slot auto-level to the Level Minimum of the five instructors. We saw 15 slots, all at Level 80. A slot on cooldown shows a 24 hour timer, and the game offers to clear it for 500 gems. A Level Reset returns the hero to Level 1 and refunds all the Hero XP.29 and 30 Oct
War AcademyFire Crystal Shard ExchangeTwo exchanges are listed. Steel gives 1 shard per exchange with “Remaining: 20”. Fire Crystals give 13 shards per 10 crystals with “Remaining: 200”. A “Till Reset” timer counts down on the panel.30 Oct
Bear HuntStamina and personal cooldownBear Hunt uses no stamina. The personal cooldown runs for two natural server days against the 00:00 UTC reset, so we tell players to follow their live countdown.29 Oct
PetsRoster sizeThe game lists 14 pets. Frost Gorilla (Generation 6) and Frostscale Chameleon (Generation 7) are the two newest.29 Oct
Frostfire MineSkill 5 branchesThe right branch is a timed gathering boost and the left branch is damage. Our sources give different percentages, so the guide tells players to read their own tooltip.29 Oct
LabyrinthUnlock requirementsFurnace 19 and a Level 1 Command Center. Sources disagree on which server week it opens, so the guide says it varies.29 Oct
Auto-JoinWhich events it coversThe panel lists three event types, each with its own “Team rewards claimed” counter out of 50: Polar Terror, Hero’s Mission and Gina’s Revenge. Crazy Joe is not listed. You can choose All Troops or a saved formation, set a queue limit (6 on our screen) and tick “Only Auto-Join After Exiting Game”. The panel says Auto-Join is available 1 minute after the rally call. Auto-Join works while you are offline. It also starts joining while you are online as soon as it is enabled, so if you call your own rallies it can use the heroes you wanted. Ticking the “only after exiting game” box limits it to when you are offline.30 Oct
Crazy JoeShields and MarksmenShields do not stop Crazy Joe. Marksmen add few kills in outgoing marches, so most players keep them home.29 Oct

Still being checked

What we are not sure about.

These are the open questions at the date of this update. When one is settled, it moves into the tables above.

  • Crazy Joe Auto-JoinSome guides say the event has an Auto-Join option. It is not listed in the Auto-Join panel on our account, which covers Polar Terror, Hero’s Mission and Gina’s Revenge. If it appears on yours, please tell us.
  • Crazy Joe detailsWhether a city gate level affects burning and random teleports is one player’s report. We also have not settled the headquarters wave ratios or the reward timing.
  • Fire Crystal exchange allowanceThe panel shows “Remaining: 200” beside the Fire Crystal exchange. It looks like a count of exchanges, in the same way as the 20 beside the Steel exchange, but we have not tested it by redeeming.
  • Hall of Chiefs pointsOur point tables come from published guides. We are checking them against the event screen.
  • Gift codesWe have not re-tested the codes labelled Reported active. If a code is Reported expired, we are waiting on our own check before calling it Expired.
  • Generation 18 heroesThe game has released these heroes, but reliable details are not available yet, so we have not filled the gap with guesses.
