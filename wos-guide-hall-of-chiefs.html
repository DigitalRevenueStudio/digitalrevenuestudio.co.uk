Hall of Chiefs:
Daily Scoring and Rewards

A multi-day scoring event for Generation 1 and Generation 2 states. This guide lists what scores on each day, how the rewards work and how to plan what to save.

Overview

What Hall of Chiefs Is

Hall of Chiefs is a multi-day competition in which each daily stage scores a different set of actions. There are three reward layers: stage milestones, daily stage rankings and a total ranking at the end. It is exclusive to Generation 1 and Generation 2 states, and later states move to King of the Icefield instead.

📅
Length

The first event is reported to last five days. Later events are reported to last six or seven, and guides disagree on which, so read the dates on your event screen.

🦸
Featured hero

Jeronimo is the featured hero in Generation 1 and Philly in Generation 2.

🏅
Rewards

Guides list gems, resources, speedups, hero experience, skill manuals, hero shards and Chief progression materials. Milestone thresholds and quantities are not published in the guides we checked.

📊
Rankings

Daily rankings use that day's points, and the total ranking uses all your points.

Scoring

Scoring Is Tied to the Day

Points come only from the actions listed for each stage. Spending an item on a day that does not score it may still help your account, but it earns nothing for the event. Points are also not the same as the items you spend. Construction and research score on the power they add, not on the speedups you use to finish them.

These tables come from published guides, and the point values match between the two we could read. Check them against the points shown on your event screen, because editions and updates differ.

The first Generation 1 event is reported to swap Day 4 for a Beast Slay day, where a Level 1 to 30 beast scores 30,000 and a Polar Terror rally hunt scores 90,000, and to have no Chief Gear day. The guides disagree on whether Essence Stones and Widgets appear in that first event.

Rewards and Saving

Rewards and What to Save

One guide says early events commonly put Jeronimo shards among the top total-ranking rewards. The guides do not say which shard types appear or how many, so check your own rewards screen. They also do not say whether any reward depends on your alliance, since both describe individual points and rankings.

  • Plan around the stages. Items such as hero shards, Lucky Wheel gems, speedups, troop promotions, Essence Stones, Widgets and Chief Gear and Charm materials score on particular days. Work out which stage scores each one before you spend.
  • Choose a target. Aim for milestones, a single daily ranking or the total board. One guide advises against emptying your stockpiles to chase all three.
  • Do not stall your account. Waiting is only sensible if the delay is reasonable. Holding a recurring benefit back for weeks costs you progress.
  • Check for overlaps. If another event scores the same action in the same week, compare the two before you spend.
  • Universal shards. Spend them on heroes worth developing. One guide lists using them only for points as a mistake.
President

What About President Skills?

Neither guide we could read says that President skills multiply your event points. Faster construction, research or training can help you complete more upgrades, but it is not a stated multiplier on every scoring action. Ask your R4 or R5 what your state plans, and check the points shown on your screen.

Community Feedback

What Players Report

These are player opinions, not guaranteed best practice.

  • Daily milestones suit most free-to-play players. Players say the overall top ranks take heavy Lucky Wheel and shard spending, so they aim for milestones instead.
  • Saving for the event. Players commonly stockpile speedups, shards and materials before the event. Opinions differ on how long is worth waiting, so weigh it against your own progress.
  • First movers on gathering days. Players suggest sending gatherers out right after the daily reset. We have not seen a source that explains the effect, so treat it as a tip.
Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Spending an item on the wrong day. Points come only from the actions listed for that day.

Counting speedups as points. Construction and research score on power gained. Only Day 7 in Generation 2 lists speedups.

Copying a schedule from the wrong edition. Generation 1 and Generation 2 differ, and the first event differs again.

Assuming a President multiplier. We found no source for one.

Emptying your stockpile for one ranking. Pick a target first.

Trusting point values without checking. Read the points on your event screen.

Check on your own screens. The length and dates of your event, the stages and point values shown each day, the milestone thresholds, the rewards, and whether speedups score on the last day. We could not read the official event page, so these figures come from two published guides.
