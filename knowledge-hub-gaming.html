Common questions

Quick answers

The questions we are asked most on this subject, answered briefly, with a link to the full guide.

How do I redeem a Whiteout Survival gift code?

On Android, open your profile, go to Settings, choose Gift Code and enter it. On iOS, use the official redemption website with your Player ID. Rewards arrive in your in-game mailbox.

Read the gift codes FAQ →

Is the Whiteout Survival Wiki free?

Yes, and it always will be. Every guide and the gift code tracker are free to read with no paywall. If it helps, players can support it with a coffee on Ko-fi.

Visit the wiki →

What is the difference between the wiki and Fathom Guides?

The wiki is a free reference you dip into. Fathom Guides are structured downloads you follow in order while you play, with a checkpoint at the end of each chapter.
