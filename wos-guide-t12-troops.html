The Basics

What Are T12 Troops?

T12 troops are the twelfth troop tier in Whiteout Survival. The game calls them Exalted troops, so you will see both names. They sit above Helios troops (T11), and like Helios they come from War Academy research rather than from your troop camps.

The easiest way to see where they fit is to separate troop tiers from Fire Crystal levels. They sound similar, but they are two different ladders.

TermWhat it meansWhere it comes from
T1 to T10The ordinary troop tiersYour troop camps, as your Furnace and camps level up
FC levelsFire Crystal levels on buildings after Furnace 30Fire Crystal upgrades. They improve troops within a tier, they do not create a new tier
T11 (Helios)The first War Academy tierA long War Academy research chain. See our War Academy guide
T12 (Exalted)The tier after HeliosFlame Tech research in the War Academy

T12 is not FC12. A troop tier and a building level are separate systems. Seeing "12" in both is a coincidence, not a requirement.

Timing

When Do T12 Troops Unlock?

There are two gates, and both have to open before you can train Exalted troops.

📅
Your state

Flame Tech becomes available at around state day 680, roughly when Generation 10 heroes arrive. Timelines vary by a few weeks, so treat it as an estimate. Our Server Timeline shows the full picture.

🏰
Your account

State access is only the start. Flame Tech opens once you have unlocked at least one Helios troop, and the T12 research itself has its own building and research requirements.

That is why two players in the same state can be months apart. One may have Helios Infantry and be working through Flame Tech, while another has not yet started Helios.

The Route

How the T12 Unlock Works

Flame Tech is the research tree that leads to Exalted troops. Each troop type has its own line, and each line ends in a research that unlocks the Exalted skill for that troop type. The Infantry line gives a good idea of the scale.

1
Unlock a Helios troop. Flame Tech only opens once at least one Helios troop is unlocked, so the Helios chain comes first.
2
Level the War Academy to FC10. The first Exalted Infantry research asks for a War Academy at FC10.
3
Complete the Molten research. The same research needs four Molten research lines (Blades, Guard, Plating and Shields) at level 5 each.
4
Research the Exalted skill. For Infantry this is Indomitable Wall. Its first level has a base research time of about 20 days, before any research speed bonuses, and costs millions of each basic resource on top of War Academy materials.

That is one research for one troop type. Lancers and Marksmen have their own routes, and there is supporting research around each one. Plan it as a project lasting months, not a single upgrade.

Plan with your own screen. Requirements and costs can change with updates. Open the research in your War Academy and tap it to see exactly what your account needs before you start saving.

Running Costs

Unlocking Is Not the Expensive Part

Getting the unlock is a milestone. Keeping a T12 army fighting is the ongoing cost. Exalted troops cost more resources to train and to heal than the tiers below, and players pushed back on this hard enough that cost-cutting research was added to Flame Tech.

ResearchWhat it doesAt its highest level (Infantry)
Exalted TrainingCuts the resources needed to train Exalted troopsUp to 25% cheaper. It stacks with the Helios training cost reduction
Exalted HealingCuts the resources needed to heal Exalted troopsUp to 50% cheaper
Exalted First AidCuts the time needed to heal Exalted troopsUp to 15% faster

These are research lines you have to complete, not bonuses every account has. Until they are levelled, every T12 troop you lose in a fight costs more to bring back.

Battle Strength

Do T12 Troops Win Every Fight?

No. A higher tier helps, but it is one part of a battle. Troop numbers, heroes, research, gear and charm bonuses, and how well a rally is timed all count as well. A well developed account with T11 troops and a full set of combat research can beat a thinly developed T12 account.

Power is not a reliable guide either. It is a score for your account, not a forecast of a particular fight. Compare the troops, bonuses and heroes on each side if you want a fair picture.

Free to Play

Should F2P Players Chase T12?

Our view: treat T12 as a long-term account project, not something to rush because the strongest players in your state have it. Before you commit, check these five things.

1
Has your state unlocked Flame Tech? If not, the best preparation is finishing Helios research and building your resource reserves.
2
How far are you from the requirements? Count the War Academy levels and research still needed. The gap is often bigger than it looks.
3
What would it stall? Months of research and resources could go into Helios, combat research or heroes instead. Decide which helps you most now.
4
Can you afford to heal them? If losses would empty your resources, wait until the Exalted Healing research is further along.
5
What does your alliance need? A reliable rally joiner with strong T11 troops can be worth more to an alliance than a weak T12 army.
Troop Type

Which Troop Type First?

There is no single right answer. Choose the troop type that fits the role you play most. Many players start where their strongest research and heroes already are, often Infantry for accounts that hold the front line or garrison, but a Marksman-focused rally joiner may get more from that line. Ask your R4s what the alliance is short of before you commit months of research.

Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Thinking T12 means FC12. They are different systems. The first Exalted Infantry research asks for a War Academy at FC10.

Expecting T12 on day 680. Day 680 is when your state gets Flame Tech. Your account still needs Helios and the research route.

Ignoring running costs. The unlock is a one-off. Training and healing Exalted troops costs more for as long as you use them.

Skipping the cost research. Exalted Training, Healing and First Aid make every later fight cheaper. They are worth planning in.

Judging fights by tier or power alone. Numbers, heroes and research bonuses can outweigh a one-tier difference.

FAQ

T12 Troops Questions

What are T12 troops in Whiteout Survival?

T12 troops are Tier 12 troops, called Exalted troops in game. They are the tier after Helios (T11) and are unlocked through Flame Tech research in the War Academy, not by upgrading a troop camp.

When do T12 troops come out?

Flame Tech, the research that leads to T12, arrives around state day 680, at about the same time as Generation 10 heroes. That is when your state gets access. Your own account still has to meet the research and building requirements.

Do you need FC12 for T12 troops?

No. T12 is a troop tier and FC is a Fire Crystal building level, so the numbers are not linked. The first Exalted Infantry research, for example, asks for a War Academy at FC10, not FC12.

Are T12 troops worth it for free-to-play players?

They are a long-term project rather than an urgent target. The unlock is slow and expensive, and T12 troops cost more to train and heal than the tiers below. Plan the route once your state has Flame Tech and your account can afford to keep them fighting.

Check on your own screens. Research costs, timers and requirements can change with updates and differ by account. Tap each research in your War Academy to see what your account needs.
