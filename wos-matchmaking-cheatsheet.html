SvS Matchmaking Cheat Sheet

Unconfirmed community tactics for lowering state power before matchmaking, with the costs and caveats.

⚔ State vs State: Pre-Match
Matchmaking
Power Reduction
Unconfirmed community tactics, usually described for the weekend matchmaking window
⚠
Unconfirmed. Guides report that the developers dispute this, and we could not confirm that it works. Some players say they get easier opponents after coordinated power drops. It also has costs: gear and hero levels have to be restored afterwards, and a state that looks weaker may lose battles it would have won. We do not recommend it as a plan. Share it only if your alliance has chosen to try it.
📅 Window Saturday and Sunday UTC Guides say matchmaking happens at the weekend. Check your event screen
01
Remove Hero Gear
Players unequip hero gear from top heroes before the window. Gear adds to individual power, so removing it lowers that number. You have to put it back afterwards.
Reported
02
Reset Hero Levels via Drill Camp
Players use the Drill Camp reset to lower hero levels. Check what a reset gives back and what it costs before you do it, because it can set your heroes back.
Reported
03
Fill Infirmaries
Players say injured troops still count towards power but cannot fight, so the state looks stronger on paper than it is. We could not confirm how matchmaking treats them.
Reported
04
Clear Inactive Player Troops
Some states clear troops from long-inactive accounts. This removes troops for good, so it is a decision for state leadership and the players concerned.
Reported
05
Coordinate Top 100 to 150 Players Simultaneously
Players say it only shows when the top 100 to 150 players act together, which is why R4 and R5 organise it. We found no published source for those numbers.
Reported
💬 How to Coordinate
1.R4/R5 agrees an exact time in state Discord or alliance chat
2.All top 100 to 150 players act simultaneously, ideally within the same hour
3.Remove gear and reset heroes at the same time so the change lands together
4.Restore gear and hero levels after the window, and check everyone has done it
⚔ Costs: Gear and levels must be restored, and a weaker state may lose fights it would win. We could not confirm any benefit. ⚠ Unconfirmed: Guides report that the developers say this does not affect matchmaking. Full SvS Prep Guide →
