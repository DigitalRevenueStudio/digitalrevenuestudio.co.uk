Is Payhip safe to buy from? A plain-English buyer’s guide

What happens when you buy from a Payhip store, who you are actually paying, how refunds work, what a PAYHIP charge on your statement means, and how to check a seller before you buy.

6 min read Digital Revenue Studio November 2026
Quick answer

Yes, Payhip is generally safe to buy from. Payhip is a legitimate UK platform that independent sellers use to run their shops. You pay through Stripe or PayPal, you get your download straight away with an email receipt, and the seller never sees your full card details. The important thing to know is that Payhip provides the shop, not the product: the seller is responsible for what you buy, and refunds come from the seller, not from Payhip.

We sell our own products through Payhip, so we see the other side of the checkout every day. This guide is for the person on the buying side: you have found something on a Payhip store, or a PAYHIP charge has appeared on your statement, and you want to know whether everything is above board.

Who are you actually buying from?

Payhip is an online shop builder. Thousands of independent creators use it to sell ebooks, templates, courses, memberships, software and some physical products. When you buy from a Payhip store, you are buying from that creator. Payhip provides the checkout, the payment connection and the delivery of your files.

That split matters. Payhip’s own help centre says it does not hold funds and cannot issue refunds directly, and that payments are processed by Stripe or PayPal rather than by Payhip. So the quality of the product, the description and the refund policy all come down to the individual seller.

What happens when you buy

  1. You choose a product on the seller’s store, or follow a direct link they have shared, and click Buy Now or add it to your cart.
  2. You enter your email address and pay, usually by PayPal or by debit or credit card, depending on what the seller has set up.
  3. You get instant access. Digital products open on a download page straight after payment. Courses and memberships ask you to log in. Physical products are posted by the seller.
  4. You receive an email receipt with your order details, a download or login link, and the seller’s contact email. You can create an invoice from the receipt if you need one.

Sellers receive what they need to fulfil your order, such as your email address and, for physical items, your delivery address. They do not get your full payment details.

How refunds work

Each seller sets their own refund policy. Some offer refunds on digital products and some do not, especially once a file has been downloaded. If something is wrong:

  • Try the simple fixes first. Download the file again, try another browser or device, and check any instructions the seller included.
  • Contact the seller. Reply using the email address in your receipt, or use the contact page on their Payhip store. Payhip’s guidance is to allow a little time for a reply, typically 24 to 72 hours.
  • Explain clearly. Say what you bought, when, and what is wrong. If the description did not match what you received, say so.
  • If you cannot resolve it, for example the seller does not reply or you were charged twice, you can raise it with your bank, card provider or PayPal, depending on how you paid.

In the UK, digital content you buy should be as described and of satisfactory quality, and faulty content should be put right. The usual 14-day right to cancel an online purchase can end once a download starts, if you agreed to immediate access at checkout. This is general information rather than legal advice.

What is a PAYHIP charge on my statement?

If you see a charge you do not recognise, it most likely means someone bought something from a seller who uses Payhip. Charges usually appear as PAYHIP*BUSINESSNAME, where the business name is the seller you bought from. To work out what it was:

  • Search your inbox for an email receipt from that business or from Payhip around the date of the charge.
  • Check the amount against anything you, or someone in your household, bought recently.
  • Remember that subscriptions and memberships charge again on their renewal date.

If you still cannot identify it, contact the seller named on the statement, and if that does not resolve it, speak to your bank.

How to check a seller before you buy

Payhip is safe as a platform, but anyone can open a store, so a few quick checks are worth it, just as they would be on any marketplace:

  • Look for a real person or business behind the store: a website, social media accounts or a history of posting about their work.
  • Find the contact page and the refund policy before you pay, not after.
  • Check ratings where they are shown. Products in Payhip’s marketplace can show star ratings from previous buyers.
  • Be wary of anything that looks too good to be true, such as famous paid courses or software sold for a tiny fraction of the usual price. Those are a common sign of copied or resold material.
  • Pay by card or PayPal, which gives you routes to raise a problem if something goes wrong.

Our view

Buying from a Payhip store is as safe as buying from any reputable independent online shop. The platform side is solid: the checkout, the payment providers and the instant delivery all work well. The part that varies is the seller, so a minute spent checking who you are buying from is the best protection you have.

Common questions

Is it safe to put my card details into Payhip?

Yes. Card payments are processed by Stripe, and PayPal payments by PayPal. The seller does not see your full card details.

Can Payhip refund me?

No. Payhip does not hold funds and cannot issue refunds directly. Contact the seller first, using the email address on your receipt or their store contact page. If that does not work, speak to your bank or PayPal.

I paid but did not get my file. What should I do?

Check your email for the receipt, including your spam folder, because it contains your download link. If it is not there, or the link does not work, contact the seller with your order details.

Do I need a Payhip account to buy?

Usually not for a single digital product. You enter your email address at checkout and get your download and receipt straight away. Courses and memberships ask you to log in so you can come back to your content.

Is Payhip a scam?

No. Payhip is a legitimate platform. If you have a problem, it will almost always be with an individual seller, which is why it is worth checking the seller before you buy.
