Refunds and Delivery

How our digital downloads are delivered, and what happens if something goes wrong.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The tools, templates and guides we sell are digital downloads. Nothing is posted to you, so there is no shipping, and downloads are not refundable.

Delivery

We sell our digital products through Gumroad, and some through Payhip. You can download your files straight after you pay, and the platform normally emails you a receipt with a link to them. There are no shipping costs and no delivery times to wait for.

Refunds

We do not offer refunds or returns on digital downloads. A downloaded file cannot be handed back, and once you have it we have no way to confirm whether it has been kept or copied. Please read the product page carefully before you buy. If you are not sure whether a product suits you, email us first and we will tell you what it does and does not cover.

If something is wrong

If a file is faulty, will not open, or is not what the product page described, please email us. We will fix it, replace it or put it right. Please include the email address you used to buy and the name of the product.

Your legal rights

This policy does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer.

Books and other items on Amazon

Where we link to a book or other item on Amazon, Amazon sells and delivers it, and Amazon’s own returns terms apply.

Contact

You can reach us at info@digitalrevenuestudio.co.uk. Our privacy policy explains how we handle your details.
