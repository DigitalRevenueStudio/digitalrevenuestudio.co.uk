🧬Research
 : ✅ : : 🆗
👑 All days: Wait for President skills before spending speedups or crystals. 🧬 Research: Day 2 is the research day. Day 5 often counts too. Follow the day your President uses the research buff. ❄ Day 3 only: Level 26+ beasts & Polar Terror rallies only. Do not start Day 2.
💡 Day 3 Secret: When leading a Polar Terror rally, send only one troop with your heroes. The troop goes to the infirmary but your heroes return instantly, letting you launch another rally straight away. Every extra rally = another 30,000 points.
