About this subject

What you will find here

Some of the most useful things we publish have nothing to do with marketing or websites. They are specialist subjects that the studio, or the brands we work with, know from the inside: a council tax band that looked wrong, a car finance agreement with commission nobody mentioned, a construction site that needs to show it is meeting CDM 2015. When a process works, we write it down so other people can follow it.

The subject is organised into topic areas. Household money and consumer claims covers council tax band challenges and car finance commission complaints, with free letter templates. Construction, site safety and leadership covers the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015, NEC contract notices and leading people on a live project, drawn from Atker Consultancy’s work in construction and infrastructure. New areas, such as street works, will be added as their own sections.

The council tax challenge is a good example of how these pieces work. It began with a conversation with a neighbour, took more than two years and ended with £1,200 paid back, and the write-up shows the checks to do first so you do not waste your time.

These pages are general information, not legal, financial or health and safety advice. Rules and deadlines change, so check the official source linked on each page before you act, and get professional advice if your situation is complicated.

Common questions

Quick answers

The questions we are asked most on this subject, answered briefly, with a link to the full guide.

Can I challenge my council tax band?

Yes. Start by looking up the bands of similar homes on your street on GOV.UK. If similar homes are in a lower band, you can ask the Valuation Office Agency to review yours, and it costs nothing to ask.

Read the full story →

Do I need a claims company for a car finance complaint?

No. You can write to the lender yourself, which means you keep all of any redress. Keep a dated copy of what you send and any reply you receive.

Get the free letter →

What is CDM 2015?

The Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 set out who is responsible for health and safety on a construction project, from the client to the contractors, and how the work is planned, managed and monitored.
