Fathom Guides Is Live: Guides Made To Be Used While You Play.

A new independent game guide brand has launched with four Whiteout Survival guides, a live updates site and a Minecraft manual in production. We are its branding and content partner, and this is how it came together.

4 min read Digital Revenue Studio Fathom Guides
Client Launch · Fathom Guides

Fathom Guides is live. It is a new independent brand publishing downloadable game guides for players on mobile, console and PC, and its first series, for Whiteout Survival, was published on 6 October 2026. A Minecraft survival manual is already in production. Digital Revenue Studio is Fathom's branding and content partner, and we are glad to see it out in the world.

What Fathom Guides Is

The idea behind Fathom is simple: a guide should be something you use with the game open, not something you read once and forget. Each guide is written as a route you follow in order, with a checkpoint at the end of every chapter so you know when you are ready to move on. The numbers in them are checked against the current version of the game, and where something differs between platforms or editions, the guide says so.

Guides are sold as PDFs through Payhip, so you buy once, keep them, and read them on a phone, tablet or computer. Games change with updates, so each guide carries an edition and version number, and a new edition follows when an update moves the goalposts.

The First Series: Whiteout Survival

The Whiteout Survival series is written from a daily free-to-play perspective. It starts with a core guide and adds three companions for the parts of the game where players most often lose value.

Starter Guide: £3.99

The Complete Event Guide. Every event from server day 1 to day 60 and beyond, the weekly rhythm, and what to save for each one. 11 pages. Buy on Payhip.

Frostfire Mine Strategy: £1.99

The patrol guard method, with the exact skill order to follow. 7 pages. Buy on Payhip.

State vs State: £1.99

Matchmaking tactics and a day-by-day prep playbook for battle day. 8 pages. Buy on Payhip.

Event Token Redemption: £1.99

Every shop, every token and what to buy with each one. 8 pages. Buy on Payhip.

All four are also available together as The Complete Bundle for £6.99, which saves £2.97 against buying them separately. Get the bundle on Payhip.

A Site That Stays Current

Alongside the paid guides, the Fathom website has a page for each game. The Whiteout Survival page carries a top tip taken from the guides, a rotating list of current gift codes and event news. The Minecraft page has a commands and cheats cheat sheet. The whole site updates from one simple data file, so keeping it current is a matter of editing a few lines rather than rebuilding pages, which suits a brand built to publish regularly.

What Comes Next: Minecraft

The Ultimate Minecraft Survival Manual is in production now. It runs to 38 chapters across seven parts, updated for Java 26.3 and Bedrock 26.50, and it is planned at £9.99. A free sample of Part I, covering the first ten minutes, the first night, tools, crafting and food, is on the way, and two companion guides on redstone and farm builds and on hardcore survival are planned for 2027.

“A route, not a wiki.”

Our Role, and What Stays Free

Digital Revenue Studio is Fathom's branding and content partner. Fathom Guides is independent, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Century Games, Mojang Studios or Microsoft. The free DRS Whiteout Survival Wiki and gift code tracker are separate and stay free, with no paywalls. Fathom is where a player goes when they want a structured, downloadable route through the game rather than a collection of reference pages.

If you have a niche you know well and want to turn it into a brand, a store and a site that keeps itself current, this is the kind of build we do. Fathom Guides is a good example of what that looks like in practice, and the full breakdown is in the portfolio.
