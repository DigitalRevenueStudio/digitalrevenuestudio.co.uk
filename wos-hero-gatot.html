Why, and when to choose differently

Gatot’s damage reflection is weapon-dependent. Defence-stat increases, shields and damage reduction must not be treated as the same percentage effect.

Common questions

Is Gatot worth building as a F2P player?

Aim towards 5★ if chosen as the main replacement. A well-developed existing Infantry can justify a staged build or selective skipping. For F2P players the suggested target is 4★ checkpoint; aim for 5★ if a planned main hero for stars and Lv.4 to 6 if used regularly for widgets.

Is Gatot good for Bear Trap?

No priority new offensive Bear project. Main PvP replacement does not automatically remove Hector or another offensive Bear leader. First skill provides Infantry Defence, not offensive joining damage.

What are Gatot's skills?

Gatot is a Mythic Infantry. Exploration skills: King's Resolve, Royal Authority, Regal Dance. Expedition skills: Golden Guard, King's Bestowal, Royal Legion. The first Expedition skill is Golden Guard.

Where can I get Gatot shards?

Lucky Wheel; General Mythic Shards, but only once you have recruited the hero; Lighthouse Intel may start dropping Gatot shards from Gen 10. Timing and amounts are not guaranteed, and the pool also includes older heroes.
