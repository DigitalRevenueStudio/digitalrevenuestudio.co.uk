How it works

Four parts. One clear brief.

A good prompt gives the assistant a task, some context and a picture of the result. The studio asks for each in turn.

1

Pick a perspective

Choose a helpful role for the assistant, or skip it. Details matter more than a grand job title.

2

Say what you need

Describe the task in plain English, then add the audience, tone, length and any must-include or must-avoid points.

3

Choose the output

Steps, a table, an email draft or your own format. The brief is built around the result you want.

4

Copy it into your AI

Edit the prompt if you like, copy it or download it, and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or whichever assistant you use.

What it does

  • Builds a complete, copy-ready prompt from your answers.
  • Lets you edit the text, copy it or download it as a text file.
  • Runs in your browser: what you type is not sent to us or to any AI.
  • Has no limit on how many prompts you build.

What it does not do

  • It does not call an AI or write the answer for you.
  • It cannot guarantee accuracy, reach, income or a particular response.
  • It does not check facts, so always verify what your assistant tells you.
  • Avoid pasting private customer or employer information into any AI service.

Questions

Good to know

Is the DRS Prompt Studio really free?

Yes. The free generator has no sign-up, no subscription and no limit on how many prompts you build. Prompt Studio Plus is a separate one-off purchase with more guided modes.

Does it send what I type to an AI or to DRS?

No. The generator runs in your browser and builds the prompt text locally. It does not call an AI and does not send your inputs to us. You copy the prompt and paste it into the assistant you already use.

Which AI assistants can I use the prompt with?

Any text-based assistant, such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. The prompt is plain text, so there is nothing to install or connect.

Will the prompt guarantee a good answer?

No prompt can guarantee accuracy or a particular result. The studio helps you give the assistant a clear task, audience, constraints and format, which usually gives a more useful first draft. Always check facts yourself.
