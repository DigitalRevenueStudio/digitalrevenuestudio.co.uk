Selection rules and limitations

Lead draft score: 40 × (stars + segments/6) + 4 × total Expedition levels + 2 × generation + 10 for Mythic + editorial Bear fit. A defensive first skill costs 80 points (64 in maximum damage focus), about two stars, unless the hero has a Bear fit weight below. Fit: Jeronimo/Hector/Mia +40; Bradley/Blanchette/Aisling/Eleanor +20; Jessie/Jasser/Seo-yoon/Gina/Patrick/Lumak Bokan/Ling Xue/Zinman −40; others 0. Trio score also adds 0.15 × available reviewed joiner scores. These are prototype editorial weights, NOT measured damage, and the tool is in beta. Override the lead trio if you have a tested team.

Joiner score prioritises first-skill level, then an editorial preference for damage dealt, Lethality, Attack, normal-attack damage and enemy-Defence reduction, then magnitude. Heroes whose first skill is offensive only on a chance, a trigger, one class, or alongside a defensive effect are ranked below clean offensive skills (they lose 150 points, so a clean skill wins unless the qualified one is about two levels higher). These stats are NOT mathematically interchangeable. Heroes whose first skill is defensive, a gathering perk or disputed (Gwen) are never picked as captains. Stars and weapon do not rank joining skills; the tool flags a joiner whose first skill is below Lv5. Joining captains are reserved before distinct-class filler heroes. No hero repeats.

Optimise for. Balanced is the default. Maximum damage focus changes the weights, not the method: heroes our notes mark as strong Bear damage dealers count 75% more, Mythic heroes get a bigger bonus (25 instead of 10 points), a leader whose first skill is a clean offensive skill gets 12 extra points, development counts about 20% less, and joiners are ranked more strongly by the type of bonus (damage dealt first). First-skill level still comes first for joiners. Because the game has no published damage formula, this is a different ranking rather than a damage calculation.

Complete leader kits, all other joining effects, gear, troop tiers, skill unlock gates, actual capacity, travel times and exact damage remain outside the model. No guaranteed four-slot selection is implied.
