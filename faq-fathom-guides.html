Fathom Guides: Your Questions Answered

What the guides are, which games they cover, how the downloads work and how Fathom fits alongside the free Whiteout Survival Wiki.

About Fathom Guides

What is Fathom Guides?

An independent brand that publishes downloadable game guides for players on mobile, console and PC. It launched in October 2026, and its first series, for Whiteout Survival, went on sale on 6 October 2026. Digital Revenue Studio is Fathom’s branding and content partner.

How is a Fathom guide different from a wiki?

A wiki is a reference you dip into. A Fathom guide is a route you follow in order, with the game open, and a checkpoint at the end of every chapter so you know when you are ready to move on. The brand line sums it up: a route, not a wiki.

Which games do the guides cover?

The range covers six games: Fortnite, Whiteout Survival, Minecraft, Clash of Clans, Brawl Stars and Pokémon, including a full Pokémon Legends: Z-A manual and Trail Ledger, a Pokémon GO web app. Each game has its own page on the Fathom Guides website.

What is in the Fortnite series?

Three guides. From First Drop to Victory Royale is the core guide for Battle Royale and Zero Build, from landing and looting to rotations, endgame and Ranked. Building and Editing Fundamentals is a shorter add-on, and Treasure Hunters: Pirate Token and Sprites is free. All three are on the Fortnite page.

Formats, Prices and Updates

What format are the guides?

Most are PDFs sold through Payhip. You buy once, keep the file and read it on a phone, tablet or computer, so it sits next to the game while you play. Trail Ledger is a web app instead: live event countdowns, a raid board with counters and a checklist saved on your phone, with one key working on up to five devices.

How much do they cost?

At the time of writing, core guides such as the Fortnite and Whiteout Survival starter guides and the Clash of Clans beginner’s guide are £3.99, shorter companion guides are £1.99, and the full Minecraft and Pokémon Legends: Z-A manuals are £9.99. The Whiteout Survival Complete Bundle is £6.99, which saves £2.97 on buying the four guides separately. Prices are set on Payhip and can change, so check the product page before you buy.

Are any guides free?

Yes. The Fortnite Treasure Hunters guide is free, and Part I of the Ultimate Minecraft Survival Manual is a free sample, covering the first ten minutes, the first night, tools, crafting and food.

What happens when a game updates?

Games change, so each guide carries an edition and a version number. When an update moves the goalposts, a new edition follows. The Minecraft manual, for example, is written for both Java and Bedrock and says where the two differ.

Who do I contact about a purchase?

Purchases are made through Payhip, and the seller handles any questions about a download. Our buyer’s guide, Is Payhip safe to buy from?, explains how payments, receipts and refunds work on the platform.

Fathom and the Free Wiki

Is Fathom the same as the Whiteout Survival Wiki?

No. The Whiteout Survival Wiki is a free reference, with every guide and the gift code tracker free to read, and it stays that way. Fathom is a separate brand for players who want a structured, downloadable route through a game rather than a collection of reference pages.

What is on the Fathom Guides website?

A page for each game, with top tips taken from the guides, gift codes where a game has them and the latest news. The Minecraft page has a commands and cheats cheat sheet. The whole site updates from one simple data file, so keeping it current means editing a few lines rather than rebuilding pages.

Is Fathom affiliated with the game publishers?

No. Fathom Guides is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games, Century Games, Mojang Studios, Microsoft, Supercell, Nintendo or any other publisher of the games it covers.

What does Digital Revenue Studio do for Fathom?

We are its branding and content partner. We built the brand identity, the compass logo and the website, and we support the guides and the live updates. The full breakdown is in the Fathom Guides case study.
