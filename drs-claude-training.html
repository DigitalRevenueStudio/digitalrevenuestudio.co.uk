Learn it by doing it

Most people try an AI assistant, get an average answer and give up. The difference is rarely the tool. It is knowing how to ask, how to check the answer and how to build the habit into the working week.

Each module takes about 45 minutes: a short read, a hands-on task using the practice files and a three-minute reflection. By the end you will have prompts, templates and checks you actually use, plus a capstone workflow of your own.
