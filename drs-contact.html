Contact

How to reach the studio, report a wiki error or ask about a product.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The quickest way to reach us is by email. For a mistake in a Whiteout Survival guide, the wiki feedback page is the best place, because it tells us exactly which guide and which screen you mean.

Email

You can write to us at info@digitalrevenuestudio.co.uk. If you are writing about a purchase, please include the email address you used to buy and the name of the product, and see our refunds and delivery page for how downloads work.

Whiteout Survival wiki: corrections and feedback

If a guide does not match your game screen, or you can settle something we have marked as unconfirmed, please use the wiki feedback page. Tell us which guide it is, what your screen shows and roughly when you checked, because the game changes. Everything we have checked and changed so far is listed on What we checked and when, and How we write and check our guides explains our process.

Working with the studio

If you would like help with branding, content or a practical tool for your own project, our services page explains what we do, and you can start the conversation by email.
