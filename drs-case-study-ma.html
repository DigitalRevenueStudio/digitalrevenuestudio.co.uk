About the brand

A character-led children’s book brand

Mavericks Adventures is a children’s book brand built around Maverick, a real cockapoo with a talent for hiding. The books are rhyming, made for reading aloud, and designed for children aged 2 to 6 and the grown-ups who read to them.

The aim is a long-running series where the character is the brand and the book is simply the first thing you can buy. Digital Revenue Studio supports the brand with its identity, website, publishing and content.

The idea

A character before a brand

The idea started with a question: could a real dog become the lead character in a children’s book series? Most publishing advice treats a book as a product to upload, price and wait on. The client wanted something bigger, with the character as the main asset and the first book as the way in.

“The character is the brand. The book is just the first thing you can buy.”
