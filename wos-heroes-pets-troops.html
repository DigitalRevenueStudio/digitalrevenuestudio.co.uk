About this section

Building your army without spending.

Hero shards, pets, experts and troops are the long term power in Whiteout Survival, and they are also where free players most often waste resources. The guides in this section share one idea: decide what you actually play, whether that is rallies, Bear Hunt, PvP or PvE, and build for that rather than building everything a little.

Heroes

Hero Priority: How to Start explains how to choose and build your first heroes. The Hero Guide by Generation goes through every generation from Gen 1 to Gen 18, with star targets for different budgets and a page for each hero. Gen 18 details are still to be confirmed as those heroes release. Hero XP explains where hero experience comes from and how to avoid spreading it too thin, and Drill Camp explains how the camp shares levels from your instructors and what a level reset does.

Pets, experts and the War Academy

The Pets Guide covers all 14 pets, how they are captured and what advancing them requires. The Experts Guide explains each expert, a sensible order for free players and how Sigils work. The War Academy guide covers the Fire Crystal era research chain. These systems open gradually as your server ages, so you do not need to plan for all of them on day one.

Troops

Troop Mastery explains Infantry, Lancers and Marksmen, the counter triangle, tiers and Fire Crystal levels, and how to plan an army that matches the way you play.

A sensible order

Start with Hero Priority so your shards go to a few heroes you will use, then Troop Mastery so your army suits them. Read the pets, experts and War Academy guides as each system unlocks for you. Our Hero Advisor, a free beta tool, can help you compare a new hero with the ones you already have, but treat its answers as suggestions to weigh rather than official results.
