How to read the verdicts

Invest The guide recommends building this hero at your spending level.

Stick with… You already have a hero in this class who does the job. A new one would not be a better use of your resources.

Skip unless Not a general upgrade. Worth building only for the specific reason shown.

Skip Not worth building for most players. Save for the next hero marked Invest.

Your spending level changes the answers, because an expensive hero that suits a higher spender can be the wrong choice for a free-to-play account. 4★ is the first big milestone for most heroes. This is advice, not a simulation, and it cannot see your gear, troops or alliance. Read the free-to-play versus spender guide.
