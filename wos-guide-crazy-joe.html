Alliance Event Guide

Crazy Joe:
Waves, Reinforcing and Scoring

Twenty waves in about forty minutes. This guide explains how the waves work, why shields do not help, how to reinforce your alliance and which troops to send.

Overview

How Crazy Joe Works

Crazy Joe sends waves of bandits at the cities in your alliance. Each member defends their own city and sends troops to reinforce allies, and both earn points. It is not a rally boss event like Bear Hunt, so do not play it that way.

🚪
Entry

You need Furnace 7 and an alliance with an Alliance HQ built.

📅
Schedule

It is reported to run on Tuesdays and Thursdays in the SvS, King of the Icefield and Hall of Chiefs weeks, and to last about 40 minutes. An R4 or R5 starts it on a schedule or by hand, and one source says they also choose which HQ it comes from.

📈
Difficulty

There are 21 difficulty levels. Higher levels bring stronger enemies and better rewards. To move up, the alliance has to clear all 20 waves and reach an alliance point target.

🛡️
Shields

Peace shields do not stop Crazy Joe. A shield will not protect your city from the waves.

One source says an alliance can run it as many times as it likes, with one running at a time and your best score kept. Both a personal and an alliance point target apply to milestones.

The Waves

The 20 Waves and What to Expect

There are 20 waves at every difficulty. Waves 10 and 20 attack the Alliance HQ, and the others attack member cities.

Most guides say waves 7, 14 and 17 target players who are online. One describes them as bonus points for being online instead. Either way, being online for those three waves is worth arranging with your alliance.

Reinforcing

Reinforcing and Being Reinforced

  • Send reinforcements yourself. Members send troops to each other's cities, and the defenders on both sides earn points. We have not seen any option that does this automatically, so plan to send marches by hand and do it before wave 1.
  • Defence points. Home defence earns full points. Reinforcement kills are reported at about half the rate. Guides describe a defence as successful when enough of the attackers are defeated, and one alliance post puts the line at 50 percent.
  • Crowding. A city with many reinforcements gives fewer kills per troop. One alliance post suggests spreading deployments evenly so one player does not take all the room at the HQ.
  • The city owner. One guide advises owners to send troops out as well, because troops left at home can take kills that reinforcers would have had.
  • Two failed defences. Guides say a city that fails twice stops being attacked for the rest of the run, and you can no longer earn defence points from it. You can still reinforce allies.
  • Healing. Two guides say not to heal troops back into your city during the event, because they will take kills. Decide this with your alliance.
Troops and Heroes

Which Troops to Send

Guides agree on the broad picture. Send Infantry first, then Lancers once your Infantry marches are full. Marksmen contribute little or no kills in outgoing marches, so players keep them at home. At the highest difficulties that may change, so check your battle reports before you settle on a ratio.

QuestionWhat guides sayWorth knowing
City wavesMostly Infantry, then Lancers. One guide gives 90 percent Infantry and 10 percent LancersA sample split, not a rule
HQ wavesGuides disagree. Two say about 90 percent Infantry and 10 percent Lancers, one says 30 percent Infantry and 70 percent Lancers for wave 10 and 60 and 40 for wave 20Ask your alliance, and use your battle reports
Where to keep your strongest teamSome guides send your best heroes with the HQ march, and one alliance post keeps the strongest team at homeNo guide shows which is better

For hero choices, guides say only that heroes who raise troop defence and health suit defending, and that you should send your strongest heroes with an HQ march. We have not found a source for naming particular squad leaders, so check the skills on your own heroes. One guide says Baldur's Expert effects can raise Crazy Joe rewards in later states, but no other source mentions it. See our Troop Mastery guide for how troop types work.

Before You Start

Before the Event

  • Agree the plan. Agree a start time, who covers the HQ for waves 10 and 20 and who will be online for waves 7, 14 and 17.
  • Pre-send reinforcements. Get Infantry and Lancers into allied cities before wave 1.
  • Activate your buffs. Raise any troop attack, defence or health bonuses and pets before the start.
  • Do not rely on a shield. It does not stop the event, and we have not found any source that says a shield blocks allied reinforcements.
  • Do not teleport mid-event. Guides say teleporting disrupts your participation, and one advises choosing your city position before the event starts.
Rewards

Rewards and Timing

Rewards listed by guides include Hardened Alloy, Polishing Solution, Design Plans, Chief Gear material chests and speedups. They are reported to arrive the day after the event, and two sources give 12:00 UTC. Rewards improve with difficulty and, according to some guides, with state age. One guide gives the contents of a chest, but we have not checked it against another source, so look at your own milestone screen.

Community Feedback

What Players Report

These are player and guide reports, not guaranteed best practice. Some come from older posts and may predate current rules.

  • Shields do not work. Several guides and players say shields do not stop Crazy Joe.
  • Burning cities and random teleports. One player who ran the event on their own farm accounts reports that a weak city can burn, and that a low-level account whose city gate has not been upgraded may be teleported at random. An upgraded gate reportedly lets the fire go out first. No other source says this, so treat it as one player's observation.
  • A zero-defence tactic. One guide describes deliberately letting low-power members' cities be breached twice so they save troops to reinforce others. We cannot confirm how well it works, and it costs those members their own points.
  • Marksmen stay home. Every guide that mentions them says they add little in outgoing marches.
Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Sending Marksmen to allies. Guides say they add few kills when sent out. Keep them home unless your battle reports show otherwise.

Relying on a shield. It does not stop Crazy Joe.

Not being online for waves 7, 14 and 17. Most guides say they are the biggest scoring windows.

Sending reinforcements after wave 1 starts. They arrive late. Send them early.

Piling everyone into one city. Crowding means fewer kills per troop.

Teleporting during the event. It disrupts your participation.

Treating a fixed troop split as a rule. Guides disagree, so check your own results.

Check on your own screens. The difficulty you are running, your milestone targets, your battle reports for troop mix, the arrival time of rewards and whether your city gate level affects random teleports. Guides were written for different seasons, so details may change.
