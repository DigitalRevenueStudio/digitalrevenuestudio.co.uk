    Books Maverick would pick

    Ages 2–5

    Spot's First Walk

    Eric Hill

    The original dog adventure for little ones. Spot sneaks out for his first solo walk and discovers the world outside. The lift-the-flap format keeps toddlers engaged through every page and makes it genuinely interactive rather than passive.

    Buy on Amazon
    Ages 4–8

    Dog Man

    Dav Pilkey

    Part dog, part police officer, entirely ridiculous, and children absolutely love him. The comic-style format is genuinely unlike anything else on the shelf and makes reading feel like a reward for children starting to read independently.

    Buy on Amazon
    Ages 2–4

    Dear Zoo

    Rod Campbell

    A classic for good reason. The repetitive structure builds anticipation and the lift-the-flap reveals keep very young children engaged through multiple re-reads. One of those books that lasts years rather than months.

    Buy on Amazon
    Ages 4–7

    The Hairy Maclary Collection

    Lynley Dodd

    Hairy Maclary from Donaldson's Dairy is one of the great children's book dogs. The rhyming text is genuinely satisfying to read aloud, it has a rhythm that carries children along and makes the books as enjoyable for parents as for little ones.

    Buy on Amazon
    Ages 3–6

    Harry the Dirty Dog

    Gene Zion

    Harry buries the scrubbing brush so he cannot be bathed, runs away and gets so dirty his family do not recognise him. A story about belonging and unconditional love, wrapped in a gloriously muddy adventure children find endlessly funny.
