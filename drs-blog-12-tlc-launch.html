Client Milestone · Atker Consultancy Services

Atker Consultancy Services has published its first book. Tactical Leadership Companion, written by Rick Shirley, is now available in hardback and on Kindle through Amazon, with a printable edition sold directly through Digital Revenue Studio. It is the first time a complete Atker resource has gone from an idea to a finished product across three formats, and it is a milestone we are genuinely proud to have been part of.

Atker has been building its reputation on project management and construction consultancy for some time. This book is the first time that experience has been packaged into something people can buy and keep, rather than something delivered through a one-to-one consultancy engagement. It sits alongside the NEC tools and the CDM Digital Audit Tool as part of the same broader project: turning years of hands-on expertise into products other people can use directly.

A Branding and Distribution Partnership

Digital Revenue Studio is Atker's branding and distribution partner. Atker brings the expertise, the ideas and the direction, and we turn that into finished products, from branding and design through to digital delivery. Everything we have built for Atker so far, including the NEC contract tools and templates, the CDM Digital Audit Tool, and now the digital edition of this book, started as an idea from Atker and was built by DRS to bring it to life.

Rick Shirley is the author name used on content DRS creates for its clients, this book included.

The Book

Tactical Leadership Companion, subtitled A Leadership Field Companion, is Rick Shirley's first book from Atker Consultancy Services. The tagline says it plainly: who can do without a little TLC? At 104 pages across 13 chapters, in a 5.5 x 8.5 inch hardback, it is built as a field companion rather than a textbook, something to reach for in the moment rather than read once and shelve.

“Who can do without a little TLC?”

It works through how you read a situation, how you decide what to do about it, and how you handle the conversation that follows, using a small set of practical frameworks that include the Challenge Ladder, the OODA Loop and the Eisenhower Matrix. None of these ideas are new on their own. What the book does is put them into a form you can actually reach for in the moments that test your leadership.

What This Means for DRS, and for Anyone Turning Expertise Into a Product

For us, this is a milestone of our own as much as it is Atker's. It is the first time our branding and distribution model has carried a product all the way from a manuscript to three published formats, using the same process we use for the NEC tools and the CDM Digital Audit Tool: Atker provides the expertise and the content, and we handle the design, the formatting and getting it in front of people who can buy it.

If you are sitting on expertise of your own, whether that is a process, a framework or years of doing something well, this is roughly what turning it into a product looks like. It rarely happens in one step. It is closer to a series of small, deliberate ones: writing it down, shaping it into something usable, then finding the right format and the right place to sell it. None of that requires the expert to also become a designer, a publisher or a web developer. It just requires the right partner for the parts that are not their expertise.

Congratulations to Rick Shirley and everyone at Atker Consultancy Services on getting Tactical Leadership Companion into the world. We are glad to have been part of it, and we are looking forward to what comes next.
