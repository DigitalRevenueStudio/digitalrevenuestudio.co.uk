Sender Review 2026: Is the Free Email Marketing Tool Worth Using?

An honest review of Sender, the free email marketing platform we use across our studio and client brands: setup, free plan, automation, deliverability and the drawbacks.

7 min readDigital Revenue StudioMarch 2026

When you run more than one brand, email gets complicated quickly. Each one needs its own list, its own welcome emails and its own automation, and most platforms start charging before you have made a single sale. Finding a tool that handled all of that without a monthly bill was one of the first practical problems we had to solve.

We tried a couple of platforms before settling on Sender. After using it across several brands, our verdict is clear: for anyone building something from zero, Sender is one of the best free email marketing options we have found.

“We needed something that worked across several brands, had proper automation and did not cost anything until money was coming in. Sender does all three.”

What is Sender and who is it for?

Sender is an email marketing and automation platform. You use it to build a subscriber list, send newsletters and set up automated sequences that go out when someone signs up, buys something or meets another trigger you define.

It sits in the same category as Mailchimp, Brevo and Kit (formerly ConvertKit), but with a more generous free plan than many of its competitors. At the time of writing the free plan covers up to 2,500 subscribers and 15,000 emails a month, which is plenty to build a real audience and test whether email works for your niche. Plans and limits change, so check the current Sender pricing before you sign up.

It is built for small businesses, creators and anyone running an online project who wants a direct relationship with their audience that does not depend on a social media algorithm.

How we use Sender across our brands

Digital Revenue Studio

The studio list is for people who want to follow what we are building. They get a short welcome sequence that explains who we are and what we do, followed by occasional updates when there is something worth sharing.

Mavericks Adventures

The Mavericks Adventures list is for parents and dog owners who sign up for the free Explorer Pack, a set of colouring pages and activity sheets featuring Maverick. The welcome sequence introduces the character, points to the books and sets expectations for future emails.

Whiteout Survival Guide

The WOS list is the most targeted of the three. Players sign up for gift code alerts and new guide announcements. An audience that specific and that engaged is exactly what email works best for.

How easy is Sender to set up?

Easy. We had a form live and a basic automation running within an afternoon on the first attempt, without having used the platform before. The interface is clean, the automation builder is visual and the form builder produces something you can use straight away.

Form builder

Drag and drop, no coding. Produces a hosted form page or an embed code within minutes.

Automation builder

A visual flow builder. Set triggers, delays and conditions without touching code.

Email editor

A block-based editor with clean templates that are easy to match to your brand colours.

Subscriber management

A clear list view with tagging and segmentation, which makes running several brands simple.

One thing to know: in our experience the form embed outputs JSON rather than a standard HTML embed, so on platforms that do not accept it you will need to link to the hosted form page instead of embedding it. That is a minor friction point rather than a blocker, and the hosted form page looks clean and works well.

What the free plan covers

This is where Sender stands out. Automation sequences, subscriber tagging, basic segmentation, the form builder and the email editor are all available on the free plan, alongside the subscriber and sending allowance above. What you give up by not paying is mainly advanced analytics, some premium templates and the Sender branding in the email footer, none of which matter much while you are building your first list.

Before choosing any platform, compare the free-plan limits of Sender, Mailchimp, Brevo and Kit side by side. They all change their allowances from time to time, and automation on the free plan is the feature to check first.

Deliverability and reliability

Deliverability has been good across our brands: emails land in inboxes rather than spam folders, which is the most important signal for a new list. The automation sequences run cleanly, with no failed sends and no emails stuck in a queue. For a free platform that is a solid foundation. As with any email service, results depend on your list quality, your sender reputation and your content, so treat this as our experience rather than a guarantee.

Sender questions

Is Sender really free?

Yes, there is a free plan with a subscriber and monthly email allowance, and no card is needed to start. Check the pricing page for the current limits.

Can I run more than one brand on Sender?

Yes. We keep each brand’s list, forms and automations separate so every audience only receives content relevant to them. Our Sender FAQ covers the practical details.
