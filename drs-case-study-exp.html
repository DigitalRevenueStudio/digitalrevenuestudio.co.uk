About the brand

An honest income project, built from scratch

The Digital Income Experiment is an independent project testing online income streams and publishing the results as they come in, including the ones that do not work. The brief to Digital Revenue Studio was to build a brand, build a website and help produce the content.

The client provides the numbers. The studio builds the strategy and writes the words.

The idea

Something people can trust

The brief was simple: build something people can trust because it has nothing to sell them. The project needed a brand that felt independent, honest and credible, and a site that was clean, fast and easy to use on mobile.
