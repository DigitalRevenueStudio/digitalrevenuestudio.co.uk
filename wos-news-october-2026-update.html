Whiteout Survival October 20 update: what is new and when.

A new event, a second arena, gifting, a new expert and a long list of quality of life changes. Here is everything, with the dates to put in your calendar.

The October 20 update (UTC) is one of the bigger ones this year. It adds the Mergeventure event, a second Expedition Arena, a gifting system, a new Expert called Justus, redesigned Chief Profiles and a long list of alliance and quality of life improvements. Several features arrive on later dates, so the table below shows what lands when.

Key dates (UTC)
DateWhat happens
October 20Major game update goes live
October 26, 00:00Expert Royal Counselor Justus becomes available
November 2Expedition Arena Season 1 begins
November 6Mergeventure event begins in eligible states

Some features and events depend on your state's progression and eligibility, so what you see in game may differ slightly.

Mergeventure: the new merge event

Mergeventure starts on November 6 and is a merge style event. You combine pieces to reach higher levels, clear fog to open up new areas, and collect Merge Coins that you can spend at the Mergeventure Caravan. Daily missions unlock further rewards. The event is only available in eligible states.

Expedition Arena: a second arena

Season 1 of the Expedition Arena begins on November 2. It alternates week by week with the Exploration Arena, so you will have a competitive arena every week.

  • Set a defensive formation, then fight using your Expedition stats
  • You lose no troops in these battles
  • Pet Skills activate automatically
  • Climb the rankings to earn rewards

Because there are no troop losses, this is a low risk way to compete. Free players can take part without worrying about wrecking their army.

Gifting system

The new gifting system lets you send gifts with a personal message to other Chiefs. Sending and receiving earns Gift Points, which raise your Gift Level and unlock exclusive rewards you can show off in your collection. It becomes available at Furnace Level 13.

Gifts are social collectibles. They do not give combat bonuses, so treat them as a way to thank alliance mates rather than a power boost.

Popularity and redesigned Chief Profiles

Chief Profiles get a new design. Other players can now give you Likes, which build your Popularity, and the new layout shows off your achievements and collection.

One detail to know: only Likes received after the update count towards Popularity, apart from eligible existing Daybreak Island Likes.

New Expert: Royal Counselor Justus

Justus joins the Experts on October 26 at 00:00 UTC. He is a former minister of the Solaris Dynasty and supports your city's development. If you are planning your Expert order, read our Experts guide first, then check how Justus fits once his skills are live.

Prestige appearances

New Prestige appearances let you customise your Chief further. They include exclusive male and female themes, eight animated emotes, special entrance animations, permanent profile backgrounds and additional Skin Stat Bonuses.

Compensation rewards

Eligible Chiefs will receive compensation for selected reward changes and additions, including Arena, Gift and Bear Hunt adjustments. Check your in-game mailbox after the update.

What this means for free players

Our take, as free players ourselves:

  • Expedition Arena is the easiest new place to compete. No troop losses means no cost to trying.
  • Free Trek Supplies twice a day is a small daily win. Make it part of your routine.
  • Hold off on spending for Justus until his skills are live and you have compared him with the Experts you already have.
  • Check your mailbox after the update in case you are owed compensation.
  • Do not chase gifting for power. It is cosmetic and social.
