Whiteout Survival Gift Codes: Your Questions Answered

How to redeem a code, why one might not work and where to catch new ones.

Redeeming a Code

How do I redeem a gift code on Android?

Open the game, tap your profile, go to Settings, choose Gift Code and enter the code. The rewards arrive in your in-game mailbox.

How do I redeem a gift code on iOS?

You cannot redeem inside the app on iOS, so use the official redemption website with your Player ID instead. Enter your Player ID and the code, and the rewards arrive in your mailbox.

Where do I find my Player ID?

Open Whiteout Survival and tap your profile icon at the top left. Your Player ID is shown under your name.

Where do the rewards go?

Into your in-game mailbox. Open it and claim them. Nothing is applied until you do.

When a Code Does Not Work

Why is my gift code not working?

Check you typed it exactly as shown, including capital letters, and that it is still listed as active. Codes can expire within 24 to 72 hours, and some can only be redeemed once per account.

Can a code be limited to certain players?

Yes. Some codes carry a restriction, such as a minimum VIP level. Our tracker notes restrictions next to the code so you know before you try.

Why do some expired codes still appear on the wiki?

We keep expired codes as a record. It helps you tell a dead code from a typing error and shows how often new codes appear.

Finding New Codes

Where do new codes come from?

Official social media, the official Discord, creator livestreams, the r/whiteoutsurvival subreddit and sometimes the in-game mailbox around major updates. Codes can expire within hours, so speed matters.

Can I redeem for several accounts at once?

Third-party auto-redeem tools exist for this. Only use trusted tools, and never share your game login with anyone. We have reviewed one honestly in our auto redemption guide.

How often is the tracker updated?

Whenever a new code is confirmed. We only publish a code once its rewards are confirmed, and every code listed as active has been checked before it is added.
