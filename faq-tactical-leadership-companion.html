Tactical Leadership Companion: Your Questions Answered

What is inside the book, which edition to choose and who will get the most from it.

About the Book

What is the Tactical Leadership Companion?

It is a leadership field companion from Atker Consultancy Services, written by Rick Shirley. It is not a textbook. It is something to reach for in the moment, with 104 pages across 13 chapters on how you read a situation, how you decide what to do about it and how you handle the conversation that follows.

Which frameworks does it use?

Practical ones you can apply straight away, including the Challenge Ladder, the OODA Loop and the Eisenhower Matrix. The aim is to give you a shape for the decision rather than theory about it.

Who is it for?

Anyone leading people on a live project or a busy site, from new supervisors to experienced managers who want a quick reference. The book comes from Atker's work in construction and infrastructure, but the situations it covers are ones most team leaders will recognise.

Why is it called TLC?

The tagline says it plainly: who can do without a little TLC?

Editions and Prices

Which formats are available?

Three. A hardback (5.5 x 8.5 inch) on Amazon at £19.99, the full book on Kindle at £7.99, and a printable edition at £12.99 in A5 for binders, with an A4 two-up version included.

What is the printable edition?

A digital version you print yourself. It is laid out in A5 so it fits a binder or a site folder, and the A4 two-up file lets you print two pages to a sheet. It is sold directly through the Atker site.

Where do I buy each edition?

The hardback and the Kindle edition are on Amazon. The printable edition and full details are on atker.co.uk. The Atker Consultancy shop page on this site links to all three.
