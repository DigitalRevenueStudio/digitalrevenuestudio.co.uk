Overview

About This Guide

This guide is for free-to-play players, light spenders and anyone wondering whether the game is worth the money. It does not tell you to spend or not to spend. It describes what the published guides say spending gets you, where they disagree and how to avoid common traps.

A caution about sources: some spending guides are written by shops that sell top-ups and accounts, so their advice has a commercial reason behind it. We weigh claims that several unrelated guides agree on more heavily than claims that appear once.

Free-to-Play

Where Free Players Do Well

Many players say alliance events reward coordination as well as spending, and that a well-organised group of active players can outperform a larger but disorganised one. Players also say that logging in daily, finishing tasks and not missing events adds up over months. These are community views. We found no guide that measures the gap in team events or shows whether free players catch up over time, so treat them as opinion and not as a finding.

One thing is clear in practice: knowing which events to compete in is free. Our Event Shops guide and the Speed-Ups guide show how to avoid wasting what you earn.

If You Spend

Where Light Spending Goes Furthest

  • Permanent over consumables. Three guides advise against raw speedups and generic resource bundles, because they are used up. Permanent benefits keep working.
  • Queues. Guides call a permanent extra queue one of the best purchases. One says the second construction queue, another says an extra march queue, so check which suits your account.
  • Monthly card. Two guides call it good long-term value. Names vary between guides, so look at what your shop calls it.
  • Passes and funds. Guides use several names. One recent guide says a progression fund formerly called the Growth Fund is now called the Dawn Fund, and only one guide mentions a battle pass at all. Check what your shop offers and read each item before you buy.
  • Packs that match a goal. One guide advises against event packs that do not line up with something you are building.
  • Sale pop-ups. A timer is a reason to pause, not to buy. Decide what you need first.
Community Feedback

What Players Report

These are player opinions, not guaranteed best practice.

  • Choose your events. Players say it is better to pick the events you can do well in than to chase every leaderboard.
  • Stay consistent. Players say steady daily play matters more than occasional big efforts.
Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Chasing every ranking. Individual boards are reported to favour spenders, so pick what you can compete in.

Buying on a timer. A countdown is designed to feel urgent. Decide what you need first.

Spreading small purchases. Several small buys of consumables leave you with nothing permanent.

Believing a claim because it is repeated. Some "gap" claims come from sellers. Check them against more than one source.

Giving up early. The gap may feel largest at the start. We cannot say how it changes later, so judge by your own progress.

Our view. Spending can save time and buy depth in heroes and gear, and we do not tell you to avoid it. We also could not find reliable data on how big the gap is in team events or how it changes over time. Check what your shop actually sells, spend within your means and use the free systems well.
