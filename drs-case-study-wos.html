About the brand

A free wiki that started with a question

The Whiteout Survival Wiki is a free-to-play guide and gift code resource. It did not start with a content plan. It started with a Reddit post that took off overnight, and a community that told us what it needed.

Every guide, every wiki page and every gift code is available without a login, a paywall or a subscription. Trust first, revenue second.

The idea

Listen first, then build

One comment shaped the whole strategy: hundreds of guides like this exist online, just not in a single wiki. The wiki is the direct result of listening to the community rather than assuming what players wanted, and corrections from readers are made the same day.
