This wiki gets better every time a player tells us something. Spotted an error? Know a better tip? Want a guide we have not written yet, or have an idea for a tool? Send it in. This page explains what you can send, how to reach us, what we do with it and how we credit you.

Get involved

What you can send us

Every guide on the wiki is written from our own play and published game information, then improved by the players who read it. Anything that would help another chief is welcome.

Errors and corrections

A wrong number, a hero in the wrong generation, an event mechanic that works differently on your screen. Tell us what the guide says and what you see.

Outdated information

The game changes often. If an event, reward or value has been updated since we last checked, let us know so we can re-check it.

Top tips

A trick your alliance uses, a better order to spend resources, a timing that works. The best tips come from players, not from us.

Improvements

A section that is hard to follow, a table that would be clearer as a list, a missing step. Small fixes like these make a big difference to new players.

Guide requests

An event, system or hero you want covered that we have not written about yet. Requests shape what we write next.

Tool ideas

Ideas for the Hero Advisor, the Bear Generator, the gift code tracker or a tool that does not exist yet.

Contact

How to reach us

Pick whichever is easiest. No account is needed and every message is read by a real person.

The feedback form

The form on the community hub asks for the guide, the type of feedback and your message, then opens your email app with it ready to send. Nothing is stored on this site.

Email

Write to info@digitalrevenuestudio.co.uk with “WOS wiki” in the subject. Screenshots help, especially for numbers and rewards.

Reddit

We read r/whiteoutsurvival and you can message u/digitalrevenuestudio. Public corrections help other players too.

How it works

What happens to your feedback

Here is what we do with every message, from the moment it arrives to the moment the guide changes.

01

We read it

A real person reads every message. Nothing is filtered out automatically.

02

We check it

We test it on our own screens where we can, compare it with published game information and ask alliance members when a second pair of eyes helps.

03

We update the guide

If it holds up, the guide is changed. Most corrections go live within 24 hours. Strategy tips may be added as an alternative route rather than replacing what is there.

04

We record it

Corrections are logged with the date on our checks and corrections page, so anyone can see what changed and why.

Credit

How we credit you

You choose

The feedback form has a credit box. Tick it and, if your feedback leads to a change, we will name you. Leave it unticked and we will not name you anywhere.

Where your name appears

In the credits at the foot of the guide you helped improve, next to the community reviewers, and on the checks and corrections page alongside the change.

Change your mind any time

Email us and we will remove your name or change how it appears. If you would rather stay anonymous, we credit “a reader” instead.

Questions

Community review FAQ

Do I need an account to send feedback?

No. The feedback form opens your own email app, so there is nothing to sign up for. You can also email us directly or message us on Reddit.

How quickly are corrections made?

Most corrections go live within 24 hours of us checking them. Anything we need to test in game, such as an event that only runs every few weeks, can take longer, and we will note on the guide that it is being checked.

What if you disagree with my feedback?

We still read and check it. Strategy often depends on your state age, spending and alliance, so a tip that works for you may be added as an alternative route rather than replacing the existing advice. If something cannot be confirmed, we leave the guide as it is and keep the note on file.

Can I become a community reviewer?

Yes. Email us with your state number and the guides or events you know best. Community reviewers read new guides before or soon after they go live and tell us what is wrong, outdated or missing.

Can I request a guide or a tool?

Yes, please do. Choose New guide suggestion on the feedback form, or email us. Requests that come up more than once move up the list.

Is the wiki official?

No. The Whiteout Survival Wiki is an independent fan resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Century Games. That is one reason player feedback matters so much: it is how we keep the guides accurate.
