CJ Rules & Guidelines: ONLINE Players • Send all infantry and lancers to ONLINE members. Keep marksmen home. Activate your pet skill for extra march capacity if needed. • Do NOT heal or extinguish fires until you've been burned twice. • Keep your 3 strongest heroes at home for defense. • HQ Waves 10 & 20: Send ONLY 85K marksmen per player. If you make it into the garrison on Wave 10, please sit out Wave 20 to give others a chance at points (unless HQ isn't full).
Send first. Check wave numbers and troop limits against your own alliance plan before sending. See the Crazy Joe guide for the full event.
Crazy Joe: Offline Players
Send second. For players who cannot be online.
CJ Guidelines: OFFLINE Players If you'll be offline but still want to earn points, send out your troops and let everyone know in AC that your city is empty and needs reinforcements. Please also look out for others doing the same! Reinforce each other's cities so everyone has a chance to earn points, even when they can't be online. CJ is a team event. Let's help each other out!
Send second.
Crazy Joe: Additional Tips
Send third.
🔥 Additional CJ Tips • Infantry get priority for kills, so send them out first! Lancers help clean up remaining enemies. • Don't overcrowd cities. Spread reinforcements so more players can earn points while keeping defenses strong. • Stronger players, please help reinforce weaker members! Keeping their cities alive benefits everyone. • HQ Waves 10 & 20: Wait until the previous city wave has landed before recalling troops. After Wave 10, return to reinforcing teammates ASAP!
Send third.
Crazy Joe: Defence Reminders
Send fourth.
Make sure your 3 strongest defensive heroes are assigned to your barricade • Do NOT teleport during CJ. You may lose your remaining wave participation. • Remember, a city only needs to defeat enough attackers to successfully defend. More troops doesn't always mean more points!

🔥 Rally Basics: Leaders, Joiners & Hero Skills Let's go over how rallies work! If another player joins with a Lv. 5 skill, it can replace the Lv. 3 skill. When skill levels are equal, whoever joined first gets priority! ⭐ What About Hero Shards & Stars? Shards increase your hero's star rating, improving their stats and unlocking higher skill levels.

A 4⭐ Jessie with a Lv. 5 skill can take priority over a 5⭐ mythic hero with a Lv. 4 skill. More hero power doesn't automatically mean a better rally joiner!

Rally Basics 4: Troops Only and the Key Point
Send fourth.
What About Troops Only? Once the rally has its 4 useful joiner buffs, additional heroes aren't necessary for those bonuses. Players joining with troops only still contribute troops to the battle and benefit from the rally's shared bonuses! 💡 The Important Part Choose heroes based on their Expedition skills, not just their overall power.
