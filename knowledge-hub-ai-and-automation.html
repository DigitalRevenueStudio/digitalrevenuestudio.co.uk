AI and automation, used sensibly.

Plain-English guidance on using AI in a small business: where it saves time, where it falls over, and how to check what it gives you.

About this subject

What you will find here

We use AI every day across the studio: for first drafts, research, reformatting, building small web tools and checking our own work. We also see where it goes wrong. It can state an out-of-date fact with total confidence, invent a detail that sounds right and produce copy that reads like everyone else’s. This subject is about the useful middle ground between ignoring it and trusting it blindly.

Everything here is written for small businesses, creators and sole traders rather than developers. You will not need to code. Where AI helped us build something, we say what it did and what a person still had to check before it went live, because that checking step is where most of the value is.

Automation sits alongside it: the welcome emails, forms and small repeat jobs that run without you. We cover the ones we actually use, on free or low-cost plans, and say when they are not worth the setup time.

This subject is new and still growing. It starts with our AI for Small Business FAQ, and the guides listed under Being written now are being added over the coming weeks.

Common questions

Quick answers

The questions we are asked most on this subject, answered briefly, with a link to the full guide.

What is AI actually good for in a small business?

First drafts, summaries, outlines, reformatting and turning rough notes into something readable. Treat it as a fast assistant for work that you then review and finish.

Read the AI FAQ →

How do I fact-check AI output?

Check every figure, name, date and claim against a primary source, such as the official page or the platform’s own documentation. If you cannot verify something, take it out.

How we check output →

Can I automate emails on a free plan?

Often, yes. We run welcome sequences on a free email plan for more than one brand. Check that automation is included on the free tier before you choose a platform, because many keep it for paid plans.
