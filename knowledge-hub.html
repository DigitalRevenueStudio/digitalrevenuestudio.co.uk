Our standards

How we write the hub

Everything here follows the same four rules, so you know what you are reading and how much weight to give it.

First-hand

We write from experience

We cover tools we use, platforms we sell on and projects we have built. When something is a client or partner story, we say so.

Checked

Facts come from the source

Prices, fees, rules and dates are checked against the official page or help centre, and we link to it so you can check too.

Kept current

Updated, and dated

Platforms change. We revisit articles when they do and show when a page was last updated.

Open to correction

Tell us if we are wrong

If you spot a mistake or something out of date, get in touch. We fix it and note the change.

Some pages contain affiliate links, which are always labelled. They never decide what we recommend. Our disclaimer explains how they work, and nothing in the hub is legal, financial or tax advice.
