Hero Upgrade Priority:
How to Start

Heroes are the backbone of your account, but the best investment depends on what you play and what you can afford. This is the place to start: how to choose who to build, how shards, XP and manuals differ, the first two generations, and when to replace a hero. Every later hero is in the Hero Guide by Generation.

The Core Rule

Build for the Activity

Your best Arena hero, rally leader, rally joiner and gathering hero may be different characters. Every hero has two sets of skills. Exploration skills shape hero combat in Arena and Exploration: area damage, healing, crowd control. Expedition skills apply effects to your troops in marches and rallies. A hero who heals other heroes in Exploration does not heal your troops on an expedition.

Concentrate scarce resources on a small number of meaningful upgrades, but do not neglect cheap utility skills just because the hero is outside your main combat team.

Resources

Shards, XP and Manuals Are Different Resources

The most common planning mistake is treating every upgrade resource as one shared pool. They are not.

ResourceWhat it doesWorth knowing
Hero-specific shardsRaise that hero's starsCannot simply be redirected to another hero
General shardsFlexible within the category they belong toScarce, so they carry an opportunity cost
Hero XPRaises hero levelSeparate from stars and skills. Drill Camp can level several heroes at once
Exploration manualsLevel Exploration skillsMatter most for Arena and Exploration
Expedition manualsLevel Expedition skillsMatter for rallies, joining and gathering. They come in rarity categories, so a Rare manual and a Mythic manual are different decisions

Starting Heroes

Molly Gen 1 Versatile Lancer for Arena and events. Several early shard sources are available, so she is easy to develop. ★ Strong Start
Sergey Gen 1 Reliable Infantry with a defensive kit, easy to obtain and develop. A sound early choice. ★ Build Early

General Mythic shard rule: flexible Mythic shards are scarce, so spend them where they unlock a meaningful milestone. A small top-up to finish a useful milestone is a different decision from funding most of an expensive hero with general shards. Jeronimo and Natalia are reported not to accept general Mythic shards, so check their upgrade screens first.

How to Upgrade

Stars, Skills and Manuals

1
Check what you need before you spend

Look at which star level unlocks the skill or skill level you actually want. Use shards to reach that milestone, then spend manuals on the effects you will use.

2
Pick the right manual type

Exploration and Expedition manuals are separate, and each comes in rarity categories. Developing a gathering skill on a Rare hero does not use the same manual as a Mythic combat skill.

3
Develop useful skills, not just heroes

A hero can be worth a useful expedition skill, such as a joiner bonus or a gathering boost, without being a priority for XP, gear or Drill Camp space.

4
Do not hold back a skill you will use

If a skill is available and useful to you, there is no need to wait solely because the hero has not reached a later star target.

Gathering and blue heroes are not wasted. Rare gathering heroes have resource-related expedition skills worth developing with the right manuals. Keep those separate from combat investment: they rarely need heavy XP, gear or Drill Camp space.

Shard Rules

How to Manage Hero Shards

✓ Do
Check which shard type a hero accepts before you spend
Use a hero's own shards on that hero
Compare the cost of the next milestone with the wait for free shards
Keep flexible Mythic shards for upgrades that make a real difference
Check your live recruitment and upgrade screens for current sources
✕ Do Not
Assume general Mythic shards work on every hero
Spread flexible shards over many heroes at once
Start a hero before you know how you will keep developing them
Copy a shard total from an old post without checking its date
Assume a build is free if it needs paid packs
Replacing Heroes

When to Replace a Hero

There is no fixed lifespan for a hero. Replace one when a developed alternative improves the activity you care about enough to justify the cost, and keep older heroes whose utility still helps, such as joiners, gathering heroes and secondary teams. A newer hero is not automatically ready to replace a developed one. Compare stars, unlocked skill levels, exclusive equipment, the mode you play and the cost to reach your target.

Community Feedback

What Players Report

These are player experiences, not a guaranteed best route. Posts can describe acquisition methods or event availability that have since changed, so compare the role, the investment assumptions and the date.

  • Flint is the most recommended early upgrade. Players favour him for his gem-funded route and as an Infantry upgrade, and many keep Molly rather than immediately funding another Lancer.
  • Advice on Philly is divided. Some skip heavy shard investment and keep Molly, some use Philly's own shards as they arrive, and others invest more when her defensive or Exploration role solves a real problem.
  • Alonso is praised for Arena, but affordability is debated. Some players commit saved general shards to him, while others save for later heroes. Both are reasonable if you know what you are aiming for.
  • Small top-ups are popular. Players often finish a nearly complete milestone with a few general shards rather than waiting a long time, while avoiding a large spend on a minor increment.
  • Older heroes stay useful. Players keep using Jessie, Jasser and Seo-yoon as joiners, defensive heroes for garrisons, Gina for hunting and resource heroes for gathering.
Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Treating all upgrade resources as one pool. Hero-specific shards, general shards, XP and the different manuals are separate decisions with separate costs.

Assuming Philly is compulsory. She has real value for some accounts, but skipping heavy investment in her is not automatically a mistake.

Writing off gathering heroes. Their resource skills are worth developing. Just keep them out of your main combat spending.

Spreading flexible shards too thin. Across four or five heroes at once, none of them reach the milestones where their skills become useful.

Copying a build that assumes paid packs. Check whether a recommended route needs Daily Deals, premium packs or a large gem reserve before you plan around it.

Replacing a hero because a new generation arrived. Compare the actual improvement for the activity you play, and what the upgrade costs.
