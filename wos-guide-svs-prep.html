State vs State

SvS Prep Phase:
The 5-Day
F2P Playbook

State vs State is the biggest event in Whiteout Survival. Most players treat the prep phase as a warm-up. The players who rank consistently well treat it as the event itself. Prep phase scoring is where a large portion of individual rank is decided before battle even starts.

The core rule: Each prep day rewards a specific activity. Research speed-ups score best on Day 2, the dedicated research day, and often count on Day 5 as well depending on the season. Using them on a day that does not score them wastes them. Hold resources, then use them on the right day.

Before It Starts

Matchmaking: Before Prep Phase Opens

⚠ Community Consensus, Not Officially Confirmed

The developers state that power reduction tactics do not influence matchmaking. However, a significant number of experienced players report consistently favourable matches following coordinated power drops. The community consensus is that it has a meaningful effect. The risk of trying is zero, the worst outcome is your normal match. Apply your own judgement.

Matchmaking occurs on Saturday and Sunday UTC. The system evaluates your state's collective power and pairs you with a comparable opponent. A coordinated power reduction during this window may result in a weaker match. Individual actions have minimal effect, this only works across your state's top 100 to 150 players simultaneously.

01
Remove Hero Gear
Unequip all hero gear from your top heroes before the window opens. Hero gear contributes significantly to power calculations and removing it creates the largest single drop in individual power.
High Impact
02
Reset Hero Levels
Use the Drill Camp reset function to lower hero levels. This reduces hero power contribution to matchmaking calculations. Can only be done once per hero per day, time it for the matchmaking window.
High Impact
03
Fill Infirmaries
Pack infirmaries with troops before matchmaking. Injured troops count toward your power total but cannot be deployed in battle, reducing your effective fighting strength relative to your paper power.
Medium Impact
04
Clear Inactive Players
Work with state leadership to identify players inactive for 20 or more days. Inactive accounts still contribute their power to matchmaking. Burning their troops before the window removes that dead weight from your state's evaluated power.
Medium Impact
05
Coordinate Top 100–150 Players
The matchmaking algorithm weighs your state's top players most heavily. Simultaneous gear removal and hero resets across this group produces a far larger power drop than scattered individual actions. R4/R5 coordination is essential.
Highest Collective Impact

Coordinate the timing so all participating players act simultaneously, ideally within the same hour. Use state Discord or alliance chat to agree an exact time in advance.

💡 Secret Tip: The One Troop Trick

When leading a Polar Terror rally on Day 3, send only a single troop alongside your heroes. The troop takes an injury and enters the infirmary, but your heroes return to your city instantly rather than marching back with the army.

This means you can launch another rally immediately without waiting for the full march to return. Every extra rally you squeeze in is another 30,000 points. Over a full Day 3 session this method can double or triple the number of rallies you lead compared to sending a full march each time.

Research Timing

Research: Day 2 or Day 5?

Alliances disagree on this, and both sides usually have a reason. Day 2 is the dedicated research day, so research speed-ups, Fire Crystal Shards, Expert Sigils and Books of Knowledge all score there. Day 5 is the widest scoring day and in many seasons also accepts most speed-up types, research included.

The split usually comes down to the President. The President can activate a Research Advancement buff, and the common plan is to spend research speed-ups while it is running. Some states fire it on Day 2 to match the research day. Others hold it for Day 5 so a large share of research lands in the final hours of prep, when matchups tend to be decided.

What to do: Ask your R4/R5 which day your President plans to use the research buff, then spend research speed-ups on that day. Check the in-game event list on Monday as well, because the schedule can change between seasons.

If you move between alliances: Follow your current alliance, even if your old one did it differently. Coordinated alliance rewards matter more than a few points either way.

Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Spending resources in the days before prep phase opens. Speed-ups, pet materials and Essence Stones used casually during the week before SvS arrive at the event depleted. Hold everything from Sunday onwards.

Using resources on the wrong day. Speed-ups used on a day that does not score them earn nothing. Research is the usual example: Day 2 is the research day, and some seasons also count it on Day 5. Check the in-game event list on Monday and ask your R4/R5 to confirm the cycle for your specific SvS.

Starting beast rallies on Day 2. The 48-hour beast event cycle means starting on Day 2 loses you one full reward payout compared to starting on Day 3. Always start on Day 3 and collect both payouts together.

Spending pet refinement materials outside the prep window. Wild Marks spent casually provide the same stat benefit but earn no event points. Save them for Day 5.

Not waiting for President skills. The President role provides a significant buff to construction, research and troop training speed. Spending speed-ups before President skills are active is one of the most common sources of missed scoring.
