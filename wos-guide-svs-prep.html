Overview

How the Prep Phase Works

State vs State (also called State of Power) has a prep phase followed by a battle phase. During prep, each day scores a different set of actions, and your total decides your individual rank. Many players treat prep as a warm-up, but the points you earn in it are a large part of your result.

📅
Length

Most guides describe the prep phase as running from Monday to Saturday morning (UTC), about five and a half days, with five scoring days. One guide says six days and another says nine, so read the dates on your own event screen.

🎯
Scoring

Each day lists the actions that earn points. Speedups are scored by the minute, and one guide gives 30 points per minute.

📋
Sources

These point values come from published guides and not from the game. They differ in places, and we mark where.

🏁
After prep

The battle phase starts on Saturday. See the short summary near the end of this guide.

Digit's Top Tip
Day 3 Beast Slay: The One Troop Trick

When you lead a Polar Terror rally on Day 3, send only a single troop alongside your heroes. A player who uses this reports that the troop is injured and goes to the infirmary, but your heroes get back to the city at once instead of marching home with a full army. That lets you launch your next rally sooner, so you can fit more rallies into the day.

We have not tested this ourselves, so we cannot say how many extra rallies it gives. Try it on one rally and check your points. A rally still has to be strong enough to kill the Polar Terror, because a failed rally wastes everyone's stamina, so agree it with your alliance first.

Shared by Slick78. Added April 2026.

The guides disagree on some details. They give different day names, and they disagree on whether Fire Crystals score on Day 1 only, on Days 1 and 5, or on Days 1, 2 and 5. Use the table to plan, then check the event list for your state on Monday.

Speedups

Which Speedups Score When

Two guides agree that a minute of speedup scores 30 points. They disagree on which type scores on which day, so this is the picture where they overlap.

The safest approach is to use construction speedups on Day 1 and hold the rest until your event screen shows the type you hold scoring. Day 5 is the widest window, so many players save a large share for it. See our Speed-Ups guide for what speedups do and when to save them.

Research Timing

Research on Day 2 or Day 5?

Alliances disagree on this, and both sides have a reason. Day 2 is the research day, so research speedups and research items score there. Day 5 is the widest scoring window and many guides list research speedups there too.

Some alliances time their research around a President buff. The President can activate buffs, but no guide we read says you should wait for one before spending, and one says any active President buff is removed when the battle phase starts. If your alliance has a plan, follow it. Ask your R4 or R5 which day they intend to use, and check the event list for your state on Monday.

If you move between alliances, follow your current alliance. Coordinated rewards matter more than a few points either way.

Preparing

Before the Prep Phase Opens

  • Stop casual spending early. Guides recommend saving speedups and event items for weeks. One says at least three weeks for free-to-play players and another says to stop casual speedup use two weeks out. Neither is an official figure, so choose what suits your account.
  • Keep progressing. Do not stall an upgrade you need only to save items. A delay of weeks costs you progress.
  • Know your day plan. Write down which items you hold and which day scores each. A short list saves mistakes during the event.
  • Save stamina for Day 3. Guides describe Day 3 as the best day for beast points, so stamina spent on beasts earlier is stamina you will not have then.
  • Pets and Wild Marks. Guides list pet items on Day 3 and Day 5. Check your event screen for the exact items. See the Pets guide.
Matchmaking

What We Know About Matchmaking

Matchmaking pairs your state with an opponent for the battle phase. Guides say it takes place on the weekend, and two say the opponent is revealed around Day 2. They disagree about what it is based on. One says it looks at the troop power of your top players, previous battle results and activity. Another says a complex algorithm favours nearby states of the same hero generation. A third says only similar power and activity.

No source we read cites an official statement. We therefore do not give a method for influencing it. You may see claims that lowering power before matchmaking gets an easier opponent. That is covered under Community Feedback below.

After Prep

The Battle Phase, Briefly

This is a short summary from guides, so check your own screens for the exact times.

  • When. The battle phase starts on Saturday around 10:00 UTC, after the prep phase ends.
  • Who you fight. Guides agree that points count only against opponents within three Furnace levels of you.
  • Shields. Two guides say attacking or joining a rally drops your shield, and that a cooldown follows.
  • Teleports and recovery. Guides mention a small number of free cross-state teleports and a field triage window for recovering troops after the battle. Details differ, so read the in-game notice.
Community Feedback

What Players Report

These are player reports, not guaranteed best practice. Some come from older posts and may predate current rules.

  • Matchmaking and power reduction. Some players report favourable matches after states lower their power before matchmaking, for example by removing hero gear or resetting hero levels in the Drill Camp. Guides we read say the developers have disputed this, and we could not confirm it. It also has costs: you have to restore gear and levels afterwards, and a weaker state may lose battles it would otherwise win. We do not recommend it as a plan.
  • Day 3 stamina. Players commonly save stamina for the beast day and lead rallies instead of just joining them. Guides we read describe leading rallies, so check whether joining scores on your screen.
Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Spending items on the wrong day. Points come only from the actions listed for that day. Check the event list on Monday.

Burning speedups casually in the weeks before. You arrive at prep with less to score.

Treating matchmaking tactics as proven. We found no official confirmation and some tactics carry real costs.

Copying another state's plan. Alliances time research and other items differently. Follow the plan your R4 or R5 sets.

Trusting point values without checking. Values differ between guides and between seasons. Read the numbers on your screen.

Check on your own screens. The length and dates of your prep phase, the actions and point values shown each day, which speedup types score when, whether joining a rally scores on Day 3 and the battle phase times. The figures here come from published guides, not from the game.
