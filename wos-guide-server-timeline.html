Overview

What This Guide Covers

Every state opens on its own day, and new hero generations, pets and systems arrive on a rough schedule counted from that day. Knowing your state age tells you what is probably next, which helps you decide whether a hero, a stockpile or a building is worth the investment now.

The day numbers below are approximate. We compared several published timelines and the game data we hold, and they do not agree for many entries. Where they differ we show a range. Later entries are the least certain, and some are estimates only. If your own screen shows something different, trust your screen.

Your State Age

Finding Your State Age

You do not see a state age counter in the game, so players estimate it. These methods are reported by published guides. Each one gives an approximation and the guides do not all use the same offsets, so treat the answer as plus or minus a few days.

MethodHow it worksCaution
VIP login daysOpen the VIP panel and read your login days total. It is a close estimate if you joined at launch and never missed a day.Every missed day makes it too low, and it does not work if you joined late.
Beast Hunt task dateFind the date the Beast Hunt task opened in your Monument, then count back about 6 days to estimate when the state opened.The offset is reported by guides, not tested by us.
Kindling Embers achievementFind the date the achievement was completed and count back about 3 days.Reported by one guide. Not available if you have not reached it.
First Sunfire PresidentFind the date of the first President in the Sunfire Castle record, then count backwards about 54 to 55 days from that date.Count backwards from the President date, not forwards. Offsets vary between guides.

Once you have a state opening date, count the days from then to today. Ask senior players in your alliance or your R5 as well, because they often know the opening date for your state.

Preparing

How to Prepare

The timeline is most useful when it changes a decision. It does not tell you to hoard everything for the next date.

  • Check what you are about to spend. If a new generation is a few weeks away and you are about to spend a lot of shards on one hero, compare that hero with the heroes in the next table row first. A hero you replace soon is a poor place for shards.
  • Keep progressing. Do not stop upgrading buildings and research because something new is coming. An upgrade you wait on costs you progress now.
  • Look at the class. The generation table shows each hero's class. That is useful when you are short of a class in your lineup.
  • Build toward the requirement. If a feature needs a Furnace level, work toward it ahead of the release so you can use it when it opens.
  • Hall of Chiefs and Season events. Some heroes come from events instead of the shard shop. Check the event guides before you spend on them.
Community Feedback

What Players Report

These are player opinions, not guaranteed best practice.

  • Release dates move. Players say the actual release in their state can sit several weeks either side of the published day.
  • Ask your alliance. Players suggest asking older members what happened in your state last time a generation arrived.
  • Read the announcements. Players say the in-game notices and the event calendar are the first place a new release shows up.
Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Treating the days as exact. Sources differ by weeks, and your state may differ again.

Counting the Sunfire offset forwards. The President date is later than the state opening, so you count backwards from it.

Holding everything for a generation. Waiting stalls your account. Keep progressing and avoid only the big one-off spends on heroes you will replace.

Assuming the same pet order everywhere. Follow the capture screen in your state.

Forgetting account requirements. A feature can be live in your state and still need a Furnace level you have not reached.

Check on your own screens. The generation and event calendar, the pet capture screen, the building list, your Furnace requirements and the date your state opened. The day numbers here are approximate and come from published timelines and our own hero data.
