About this subject

What you will find here

Every brand we run sits on a small stack of free or cheap tools: static hosting, a domain, an email platform, a storefront and analytics. We have tried enough of them to know which ones earn their place, which ones quietly cost more than they save, and when it makes sense to start paying.

We also build tools. The CDM Digital Audit Tool and the NEC Contract Manager started as real problems on construction sites, where signal is poor and paperwork piles up, and became browser-based apps that work offline. Their FAQs explain what they do and, just as important, what they do not replace.

Start with our free tool stack if you are building a site or a brand from scratch. It lists every tool we use, what it costs and what we would change.

Common questions

Quick answers

The questions we are asked most on this subject, answered briefly, with a link to the full guide.

Can I host a small business website for free?

Yes, at launch. A static site on free hosting is fast, secure by default and cheap to run. You pay for the domain, and move to paid options only when the business needs more.

Read the website FAQ →

Which analytics should a new site use?

Google Analytics 4 for how visitors behave and Google Search Console for indexing and search visibility. Both are free, and we put both on every site from day one.

See the full tool stack →

Do the CDM and NEC tools work offline?

Yes. Both run in a browser with nothing to install and keep working on site without a signal once they are loaded. Audits and notices can be exported when you are done.
