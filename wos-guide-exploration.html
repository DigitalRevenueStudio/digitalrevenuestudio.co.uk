Heroes and Exploration

The Basics

What Is Exploration?

Exploration is the hero-only battle mode you open from the Explorers Cabin in your city. You pick a lineup of five heroes and fight through a long run of stages, one at a time, without any troops. It opens early in the game and keeps going for as long as you play.

It matters because of what it pays. Every stage you clear raises the idle rewards the cabin collects while you are away, and those rewards feed the rest of your account.

RewardWhere it comes fromWhy it matters
Hero XPIdle rewards and stage clearsLevels up every hero you use, in every mode
SteelIdle rewards and stage clearsNeeded for research and several city upgrades. Exploration is the steady free source
Hero shards and skill manualsBoss stages, which come round regularlyHelp push stars and skills on the heroes you are building
GearJoins the idle rewards much further into the stagesMostly lower-tier pieces, useful as upgrade material

Collect often. Idle rewards stop building after about nine hours. Collecting two or three times a day means nothing goes to waste.

The Key Rule

Exploration Skills vs Expedition Skills

This is the single most useful thing to understand about Exploration. Every hero has two separate sets of skills, and only one of them counts here.

🗺️
Exploration skills

Used in hero-only battles: Exploration stages and the Arena. These are the skills that decide whether you clear a stage.

⚔️
Expedition skills

Used when heroes lead troops: Bear Trap, rallies, SvS, attacks and defence on the map. They do nothing in Exploration.

This explains a lot of confusion. A hero who is brilliant in Bear Trap can be ordinary in Exploration, and the other way round. Before you spend manuals to get past a stage, check that you are upgrading the hero’s Exploration skills, not the Expedition ones. Each hero guide lists both.

Matchups

The Class Triangle

Exploration battles follow the same three-way counter system as the rest of the game. The class with the advantage deals roughly 10% extra damage to the class it counters.

ClassBeats / loses toUsual job
InfantryBeats Lancers, loses to MarksmenFront row. Soaks up damage and protects the back row
LancerBeats Marksmen, loses to InfantryFlexible. Damage, support or a second front-liner
MarksmanBeats Infantry, loses to LancersBack row. The main damage dealers

Ten percent sounds small, but it applies to every hit in the fight. Against a stage that is mostly one class, turning the matchup in your favour is often worth more than a few levels of raw power.

Lineups

Setting Up Your Team

1
Two in front, three behind. The lineup has two front-row and three back-row slots. Put your toughest heroes, usually Infantry, in the front row.
2
Protect your damage. Put Marksmen and other damage dealers in the back row, where the front row shields them.
3
Read the enemy first. Look at the classes in the stage before you fight and lean your lineup towards the counter.
4
Do not trust quick deploy blindly. It picks your five highest-power heroes, which is not always the team that wins the matchup.
When You Hit a Wall

Stuck on a Stage? Try This

1
Watch where it goes wrong. Does your front row fall first, or does the fight just run out of damage? That tells you which row to change.
2
Check the enemy’s main class. Swap in heroes that counter it, even if their power is a little lower.
3
Change one hero at a time. Changing the whole team at once makes it hard to tell what helped.
4
Upgrade the right skills. Spend on Exploration skills for the heroes you actually use here, not Expedition skills.
5
Level up from your idle rewards. The Hero XP the cabin collects is there to level your Exploration team.
6
Try again. Results can vary a little between attempts, so a near miss is worth a second go.
7
Come back later. If you are well short, collect rewards for a few days. A level or star upgrade elsewhere often clears it.
Common Mistakes

What Most Players Get Wrong

Upgrading the wrong skills. Expedition skills do nothing in Exploration. Check which skill set you are spending on.

Chasing power instead of matchups. A well-matched team often beats a stronger one that is countered.

Letting idle rewards cap. After about nine hours the cabin stops collecting. Little and often wins.

Spending premium items to force a stage. Exploration rewards build steadily over time. It is rarely worth paying to rush one stage.

Building heroes only for Exploration. Shards are limited. Build heroes for the activities that matter most to your account, as our hero priority guide explains, and fit Exploration around them.

FAQ

Exploration Questions

Why am I stuck on an Exploration stage?

Usually because of the matchup, not raw power. If the enemy team is mostly one class, a lineup built to counter it often wins where a stronger but badly matched team loses. Check the enemy's main class, swap one hero at a time and watch where the fight goes wrong.

Do Expedition skills work in Exploration?

No. Heroes have two separate skill sets. Exploration skills are used in hero-only battles such as Exploration and Arena, and Expedition skills are used when heroes lead troops, for example in Bear Trap, rallies and SvS. Upgrading an Expedition skill will not help you clear an Exploration stage.

Which class beats which in Exploration?

Infantry beats Lancers, Lancers beat Marksmen and Marksmen beat Infantry. The class with the advantage deals roughly 10% extra damage to the class it counters.

How long do Exploration idle rewards build up?

Idle rewards fill up over about nine hours and then stop. Collecting at least two or three times a day means nothing goes to waste.

Does Exploration give Steel?

Yes. Steel is one of the idle rewards from the Explorers Cabin, alongside Hero XP, and clearing stages adds more. Pushing further through the stages raises the amount you collect, which is why Exploration is the steady free source of Steel for most players.

Every account is different. Stage difficulty, rewards and unlocks can change with updates. Use this guide to understand how Exploration works, then check your own Explorers Cabin.
