How to redeem

Three ways to claim your rewards

Use whichever suits your device.

01

In game

Android
  1. Open Whiteout Survival
  2. Tap your profile icon
  3. Go to Settings
  4. Tap Gift Code
  5. Enter the code and confirm

iOS players cannot redeem in the app. Use method 2.

02

Official website

iOS and all devices

Visit wos-giftcode.centurygames.com, enter your Player ID and the gift code. Rewards arrive in your in-game mailbox.

Your Player ID is under your name on your profile screen.

03

Auto redeem tool

All devices

If you manage several accounts, tools like WOS Rewards save your Player IDs and redeem a code for all of them at once.

Only use trusted tools. Never share your game login with a third party. Read our honest review.

Quick answers

Gift code questions

How do I redeem a Whiteout Survival gift code?

On Android, open the game, tap your profile, go to Settings, choose Gift Code and enter the code. On iOS you cannot redeem in the app, so use the official redemption website with your Player ID instead.

What do the status labels mean?

Verified active means we redeemed the code recently. Reported active means players and code sites say it works but we have not re-tested it. Reported expired means players and code sites say it has ended. Expired means it is confirmed as no longer redeemable.

Why is my gift code not working?

Check that you typed it exactly as shown, including capital letters, and what its status label says. A code marked reported active has not been re-tested by us. Codes can expire within 24 to 72 hours, and some can only be redeemed once per account.

Where can I find my Player ID?

Open Whiteout Survival, tap your profile icon at the top left and your Player ID is shown under your name.

How often is this page updated?

Whenever a new code is confirmed or a status changes. We only publish a code once its rewards are confirmed, and we redeem new codes ourselves when we can. After that, codes we cannot re-test are labelled reported active or reported expired, depending on what players and code sites say.

Where do rewards go once I redeem a code?

Rewards arrive in your in-game mailbox. Open the mailbox and claim them.
